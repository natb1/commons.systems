Stage 1 — weekly pace curve W(x)

Cumulative weekly budget you're allowed to have spent by now. The router throttles whenever actual used_weekly runs above this curve — even far below the weekly cap. The curve holds a constant weekly_pace_floor early in the week, then rises (power p) to the derived shoulder as the final weekly_terminal_windows approach, then climbs the linear terminal segment (slope = cap per remaining window) to weekly_terminal (100% by default) at week close. Monotone by construction. X-axis is 5-hour windows elapsed (0 → 33.6).

effective W = max(floor, rise, terminal) rise (floor→shoulder) terminal segment (slope = cap/window) used_weekly (actual) now (current window) over-pace → paused
