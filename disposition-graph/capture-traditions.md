## Proposal

Candidates proposed by the AI on 2026-09-02 from its own knowledge, each to become a reading under the capture node once the author chooses. None has authority yet.

- Stigler, "The Theory of Economic Regulation" (1971): regulatory capture, the regulator coming to serve the regulated.
- Jensen and Meckling, "Theory of the Firm" (1976), and Ross, "The Economic Theory of Agency" (1973): agency costs and the principal-agent problem.
- Hirschman, Exit, Voice, and Loyalty (1970): the two responses to a declining institution; already cited under the archē.
- Illich, Tools for Conviviality (1973): institutions past a threshold become counterproductive and monopolise the ways a need can be met.
- Simon, "Designing Organizations for an Information-Rich World" (1971), and Wu, The Attention Merchants (2016): attention as the scarce resource institutions capture.
- Zuboff, The Age of Surveillance Capitalism (2019): platforms that model and steer intention.
- Frankfurt, "Freedom of the Will and the Concept of a Person" (1971): acting on desires one does not endorse, capture described from the inside.
- Pettit, Republicanism (1997): domination as the capacity to interfere arbitrarily; already a reading under the archē.
