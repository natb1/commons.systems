**AI support.** Recorded in the tradition pass on the deferral mechanism, 2026-09-08. The objection transfers almost without adjustment. Deference to a drafter's reading of its own text is criticised because one party writes the instrument, controls how clear it is, and then supplies the reading that governs when it turns out to be unclear; the incentive runs toward ambiguity, and no bad faith is required for it to operate. In this record the AI drafts the probe, controls how clear the probe is, and supplies the option that becomes the author's unconfirmed choice when the author says the probe is not clear to them. Manning's structural point, that the writer of a rule should not be the one who says what it means, and Scalia's compressed version of it, that he who writes a law must not adjudge its violation, name the arrangement exactly.

It bites harder here than in the setting it comes from, and the reading says so rather than softening it. In the administrative case the drafter and the deferring party are different institutions, and the incentive runs through an agency that gains only indirectly. Here they are one party, and the gain is direct: each incomprehension moves a class in the direction of the party that wrote the sentence that was not understood. There is also no independent measure of how clear a probe could have been, so nothing in the record distinguishes a hard question honestly put from a question put hard, and a systematic tilt would leave no evidence of itself.

The divergence is Kisor-shaped, and that is what the record takes. Kisor had the option of overruling Auer and declined it, on the ground that the deference does real work where the drafter genuinely knows more, and fenced it instead with conditions: the ambiguity must be genuine after the interpretive tools are exhausted, the reading must be reasonable, it must be the drafter's authoritative and considered position rather than a convenient one, and it must fall within the drafter's competence. `the-choice-is-the-record-read-back` installs the analogues rather than the refusal. The reading must be read off the author's own recorded dispositions and say which ones, which is the exhaustion condition. The rest of the response is the scope and binds, which is the reasonableness condition given content. Where the scope admits two readings the narrower one governs, which is contra proferentem doing the work Kisor's "authoritative and considered" does, and which is the one clause that cuts against the drafter at the moment of reading. And the class is ruled by the author in words that name the probes, which is what makes the position the author's considered one rather than an inference.

**AI divergence.** Kisor's conditions are applied by a court that did not write the rule, and every analogue here is applied by the AI. The exhaustion condition is checked by the party that would benefit from finding the record silent; the narrower-reading rule is applied by the party it runs against; and the author's ruling, which is the one condition administered from outside, is a ruling on a question the AI framed. So the fence is real in its content and self-administered in its operation, and a reader who found that insufficient would be applying the tradition more faithfully than this record does. The record's answer is not that the objection fails but that the alternative is worse: refusing the mechanism leaves the author's deferral unrecorded, which loses a ruling they made and blocks the node on a question they have said they will not answer.

## Answer

The objection is structural and needs no bad faith to operate: where one party
writes an instrument, controls how clear it is, and then supplies the reading that
governs when it is found unclear, the incentive runs toward ambiguity and the
functions that should check each other are merged. The deferral mechanism has that
shape and has it in a sharper form, because the drafter of the probe, the proposer
of the option, and the party the authority moves to are one party rather than two
institutions, and no measure exists of how clear a probe could have been.

The record diverges from the objection's strongest form, which is that such
deference should not exist, and follows the course taken when the same question was
put to a court that could have abolished it and did not. The deference is kept
because it does work the alternative cannot do, and it is fenced: the AI's reading
must be taken from the author's recorded dispositions and say which ones; the rest
of the response is the scope and binds; where the scope admits two readings the one
leaving the AI less room governs; and the class moves only on the author's own words
naming the probes they answer.

What the record does not claim is that the fence is administered from outside. Each
condition but the last is applied by the party it constrains. The divergence is
recorded so that a reader can hold the record to the difference.
