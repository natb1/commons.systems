## Answer

The field's negative result on keeping a record of the options, and the reason
that result does not reach a record whose keeper is an AI. Grudin's first
challenge is that groupware fails where the party who must do the extra work is
not the party who gets the benefit, and structured design rationale is the
field's own textbook instance of it: the designer pays, in interruption and
bookkeeping, at the moment of deciding, and the beneficiary is a reader months
later who may never exist. Buckingham Shum and Hammond put the question in their
title, what use at what cost, and the field's answer across a decade was that the
cost fell in the wrong place. The practice was demonstrated in research settings
and abandoned in industry.

The constraint that shelved it was who holds the pen. Every one of the five marks
this record's answer puts on a row is bookkeeping: which named expert chose which
option, an author's selection kept distinct from a confirmation, a tradition's
support pinned to the option it bears on, and all of it kept current as the text
moves underneath. A human record-keeper could not afford that per option per fact
per node, and the field concluded from the unaffordability that the practice was
wrong. Here the maintainer is the AI and the reader is the author, so the
disparity does not merely shrink, it inverts: the party paying the capture cost is
not the party whose attention is scarce. That is the licence to adopt a retention
discipline the field shelved, and it is why `viable-options` can keep every
candidate the AI considered and can name.

What does not come back with the licence is the other half of the finding, and
this reading is the sharpest statement the record has of it. The diagnosis had two
parts, and only one was about capture. The other was that argumentation-based
representations imposed a cost on the reader that rose with the notational
richness of the representation, and that cost was always the reader's, which here
is the author's. So these are two costs with two payers, and an argument that
disposes of one disposes of nothing about the other. The record's working
distinction between what it retains and how it draws what it retains is this
finding restated, and the arrival of an AI record-keeper answers the capture half
and leaves the reading half exactly where the field left it — on the half the
recommendation adds to.
