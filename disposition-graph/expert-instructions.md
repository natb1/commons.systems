#### fragments-and-a-builder

No expert skill: the durable artifact is a set of composable fragments and a
builder that assembles one brief per launch, on the model the record already
runs for its reviewers, where `packages/clean-context-review/brief.mjs` composes
`brief-bounds.md`, `brief-draft.md`, `brief-survey.md`, `brief-delta.md` and
`brief-record.md` into the text a reading is given. The expert loads no skill and
reads one generated brief. It is distinct from a skill in what it makes possible:
a launcher can compose a different brief for each of two experts convened on the
same question, which a single skill cannot, and the parts are separately
readable and separately amendable.

**AI support.** The record has already solved this exact problem once, for the
party the expert is replacing, and the instrument exists and is tested. It is the
only option that makes deliberately-different instructions cheap, which is what
`how-many-experts-and-do-they-read-the-same-brief` may require: Knight and
Leveson 1986, already a reading in this record and cited by `movements`' own
`who-convenes-the-experts`, holds that independence must be demonstrated and is
never inferred from separate construction, and identical briefs are a
correlation by construction. Fragments also localize the damage: a floor list
that fixates is one fragment a launch can decline to include.

**AI divergence.** It gives up the author's stated form and the third reason with
it: instructions assembled into a prompt are not a skill and are not faceted by
skill in any telemetry that facets by skill, so `words/2026-09-08/30`'s third

an expert instructed to respond with references to tradition must reach outside
the record, so the nearest existing fragment is in direct conflict and would be
forked rather than shared, which removes most of the reuse the option is argued

### authority

Ratified, on the capture-shaped limb of `class-recommendation`'s test, and on
that limb alone.

Capture-shaped, and this is the limb: the text an expert is given is written by
the party whose recommendation the expert exists to test. The expert paradigm's
whole claim, in `movements`' refinement of 2026-09-08, is that convened experts
raise probes the AI did not raise, and the party that would set what they are
told is the party whose blind spots they are convened to find. `movements` names
the same asymmetry as an open probe of its own, `who-convenes-the-experts`, which
asks whether the party selecting the families and writing the briefs may be "the
AI whose recommendation they exist to test", and cites Knight and Leveson 1986
for the result that independence must be demonstrated and never inferred from
separate construction. `growth`'s authority fact reads ratified on the same limb
for the same structural reason, that the party which would set the conduct of the
interview is the party the conduct exists to check. The evidence that the limb is
live here is not hypothetical: on this node's own account, five expert briefs of
2026-09-08 supplied floor lists of loci, two supplied the AI's own best finding
inside the framing, and the tradition survey that produced this node's
counter-evidence was given nine families and returned nine.

Expensive: not met. A wrong standard costs the returns of the sittings that ran
under it, and those are re-runnable; nothing the record cannot take back cheaply
is spent.

Irreversible: not met. The artifact is a file, and a wrong version is rewritten
in one edit and one landing. Nothing is deleted, swapped, or built on.

Boldness low on the class: the reading applies the stated test, one limb is met
and the other two are named as not met, and the sibling node's own open probe is
the record's own statement of the limb rather than the AI's.

### What the sitting's own briefs actually said

The empirical object of this question is the corpus of briefs this sitting wrote
by hand before the author ruled that it should not have. Thirteen were extracted
verbatim from the sitting's transcript on 2026-09-08. Five were expert briefs in
the new paradigm, two were record surveys, three were read-only research units,
two were code units with verification contracts, and one was a design brief. The
anatomy below is measured over all thirteen and is the reason this node exists;
the file it was extracted into is gitignored scratch and dies with the job, so
what it found is recorded here.

**What recurred.** A write bound, in all thirteen. A prescribed output structure,
in eleven. "Report the exact commands you ran", in twelve. A stated effort, in
ten. The refusal of a verdict, in five -- exactly the five expert briefs. A
demand for citations with a checkable locus, in five. A demand for probes put
open and never as a choice between drafted answers, in five, in three wordings.
"What an answer would settle, and why the record cannot answer it", in five. "The
one thing you think the AI has not noticed", in seven -- the five experts and the
two surveys. A floor list of traditions to cover, in five of five. "Do not paste
file contents; the main thread reads the conclusion", in six. A statement of
where the record is and how a node file is shaped, in six. The node binary's
absolute path, in five. The warning that the projector crashes when run from
inside `disposition/`, in four.

**What is genuinely per-launch.** The question and its ledger address; the
tradition family and what is being proposed; the record files that ground the
incumbent; the deliverable path and the files the unit may write; the worktree
and the graph pin; the model and the effort; and the question-specific sections
of the return.

**What varied that no one chose to vary.** This is the measured defect, and every
item in it is in the contract half of a brief.

- Error behaviour was stated in **one** brief of thirteen: "if you cannot read
  something you need, say so under a heading 'What I could not read' and
  continue. Never guess at file contents." `delegation`'s answer requires a
  unit's error behaviour in its contract, and
  `delegation-bounds-and-sizing` places that clause among the bounds and not
  among the sizing.
- The model was named in the harness's own field for ten of thirteen and left at
  the default for three. `delegation`: "a subagent is never launched on the
  harness's default, since a default is not a sizing and a brief that leaves the
  model unnamed has not sized the work."
- The write bound was written **nine different ways**, and the enumerations of
  the forbidden version-control verbs disagree: one brief forbids
  add/commit/push/checkout/reset/stash, another commit/checkout/add/stash/branch,
  a third add/commit/checkout/stash/push/worktree. `reset` appears once,
  `branch` once, `worktree` once.
- The record's own file format was stated **three mutually inconsistent ways**,
  and the version carried identically by three briefs was false: it described
  `## Question`, `## Answer`, `## Facts`, `## Account` and `## Readings`
  sections, which is the legacy encoding the record left on 2026-09-04. Three
  units ran under a wrong map of the encoding and their findings had to be
  re-checked. This is the single most expensive defect in the corpus and it is
  the one a durable text most obviously prevents.
- "Report the exact commands you ran" was omitted from exactly one brief: the
  tradition survey on standardized instruments, whose return is ~45 citations
  someone would have to check, and which was permitted web search. The clause
  was present in the twelve briefs where it mattered least.
- The instruction to mark each probe periagogic or maieutic appeared in **one of
  the five expert briefs**. It is one of the two things `words/2026-09-08/27`

- "Traditions shelved by pre-agent constraints are in scope" appeared in two
  briefs and in one of the five expert briefs. `evaluation`'s answer requires it
  of every evaluation.
- The ledger address of the author's words was given correctly in three briefs,
  abbreviated to bare entry numbers in one, and omitted in one -- the tradition

- "Do not paste your context; report the conclusion" appeared in six of thirteen.

evidence about where the AI biased its experts, which is the material argument
against standardizing the tradition-facing half rather than against
standardizing at all.

- Every one of the five expert briefs supplied a floor list of tradition families
  or loci to cover. The survey on standardized instruments was given nine
  families as "a floor, not a ceiling" and returned nine families that track the

  distinguishing feature is that an expert is given the question and not the
  draft, and the first two briefs written under it were draft-first briefs with a

### The case against the disposition, on its own three grounds

*Against consistency.* Variance compression is the defect and not the benefit,
