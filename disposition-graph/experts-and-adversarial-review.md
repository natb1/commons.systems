### The record survey, 2026-09-09, of 39570ced

The survey unit read the record at graph commit 39570ced for what adversarial review does in it and whether dialogue with experts fills each function; what it returned is written here as data, since its report stood outside the record and dies with the sitting. The record uses "adversarial review" in three senses, the AI's review of its own recorded output as part of producing it, the clean-context review in its two readings, and the diff review of a materialized landing; the author's question at `words/2026-09-08/26` is about the second, and that is the one the survey read.

**The fill test.** Adversarial review performs eleven distinguishable functions in the record: the wind-egg test, the review of one's own output as part of producing it; the purgation of the drafter's conceit, the function no party discharges on itself; the verdict that gates the author's ruling, with the readiness gate; the steelman, the case against written by a party that did not draft; the inspection of the artifact as written, for defects of composition; the frontier's self-consistency; the per-draft validations, the first six and the fifteenth, over one node and its neighbourhood; the pin, by which a reading records the text it read so that staleness is computable; the kickback, a durable node-moving verdict with residue; the gate that fires without the author spending attention; and the diff review of a materialized landing. Dialogue with experts fills the first, unaffected; partly fills the per-draft validations; does not fill the other eight, the kickback by design; and the diff review is not at issue, though its dependency on the clean-context reviewer breaks. The record's own division, written before this survey in the entry "What is lost in the strike, both experts, 2026-09-08" on `clean-context-review`, a dialectical function the expert system subsumes and dominates and an inspection function it does not, survives the enumeration and is confirmed by it; what that entry did not do is enumerate.

**What is lost.**

1. The inspection function, the one the record names as unsubsumed on two groundings. `clean-context-review`'s recommended text: the reading in clean context is "the one function the expert system does not subsume, since a scoped reader cannot by construction find a defect of composition -- a contradiction between two facts each well supported, a node correct and redundant, vocabulary drift across siblings"; its account adds the mechanism, that "an expert convened on a fact argues about the fact, while a reader in clean context is the only party whose object is what the record actually says rather than what the parties meant it to say". The record's worked example is `growth`, whose frontmatter read `status: passed` while its account still read "Adopted into the recommendation on 2026-09-07": a disagreement between two parts of one document, which is not an argument about anything.
2. The party who is not the drafter. `plato-elenchus`: "This is the function no party can discharge on itself... Its authority comes from the reviewer not being the drafter, and not from what it finds." Under the paradigm the AI selects the families, writes the briefs, reads the returns and holds the record, `movements`' probe `who-convenes-the-experts` recording that each of the two surveys of 2026-09-08 named itself as the instance, and the author's answer at `words/2026-09-08/39` puts the main thread on the checking side too. The review paradigm shares part of this defect, `clean-context-review`'s fourth independence clause being stated and unmaterialized, but it has three clauses that bind and one that does not, where the expert paradigm has no clause here at all.
3. The steelman written by someone else. `anchoring-and-adjustment` names three guards, the second being the clean-context review "whose counter-argument replaces the AI's own on the row and is written by a reader that did not draft it"; `movements`' probe `who-writes-the-against-after-the-encoding` finds that under the paradigm the probes return before the option rows exist, the AI then writes the row, and the against reverts to the drafter's own, so the paradigm strengthens the third guard and deletes the second. Discharged by "I defer", with a discharge reason that says the deferral does not decide between a small instrument at the encoding and the AI's own case against.
4. The frontier's only reader. `frontier-consistency`: "inconsistency within the frontier has no author to meet it, and the review is where it is surfaced". Validations seven to sixteen run against the whole graph; no expert in the record is convened on the frontier, every one of the six expert-choice blocks at HEAD naming a single answer fact; 155 nodes carry a survey pin and 57 findings stand as its residue.
5. The pin and everything computed from it. At HEAD the option sources are `ai` 520, `review` 242, `author` 111, and node ids, and no `expert`: there is no way to record that an expert said anything, so `recording`'s readiness gate, both readings pinning the recommendation as it stands, is unsatisfiable by dialogue, and three of the alignment page's six metrics lose their input. `expert-identity`, which would fix this, is at the periagogic stage.

Both experts of 2026-09-08 named it, `dialectical-conduct` asks that it be counted as a transfer and not a saving, and nothing counts it.

**What is gained, and whether it is better.**

1. Volume and quality of argument, on the record's own concession, written by an expert convened to hunt for losses: "The expert system supplies far more argument than one reviewer ever did, and on quality of argument the experts plainly dominate."
2. Refutation at the right moment, which is Plato's argument at `plato-elenchus`: "a review placed only at the end of a sitting arrives after the moment refutation was for". Its corollary, from `movements`' own probe, is that nothing in the record says this competes with a reading of the artifact at the end: an argument for adding early dialogue and not for striking the late reading.
3. Tradition resolved where the decision is made, by `readings`' per-edge support and divergence and `dialogue`'s `an-option-accumulates-per-expert`, which the review never offered.
4. Absence made recordable, `unreached-traditions`: "no other party can say what it did not open".
5. The assigned-advocate defect removed, after Nemeth, Brown and Rogers 2001, that assigned advocacy can bolster the position it opposed; but the constituted reading also assigns no adversarial role and supplies no candidate finding, so this does not discriminate between the paradigms.
6. A measurement running the other way, on `llm-systems-evidence`: a single-pass frontier model beat two multi-agent debate systems by 0.66 and 0.57 rank points with 95% intervals excluding zero, the losing system at about thirty times the tokens (arXiv 2607.14713); and that node's ninth probe finds no measurement in any medium that an ensemble of scoped readers recovers composition defects no member could see, which is the load-bearing empirical claim under the strike.
7. The review's own cost, stated by the record against itself in `recording`'s against: every ruling waits on two readings of the AI's own drafting, and where the cap has been reached the rule bends, so the author's last sight of the text is one no reader saw.

The claim that the expert paradigm is better holds on the dialectical half, argument, timing, tradition resolution, recordable absence and ruling throughput, and is not established on inspection, independence or cost: on inspection the record says the function is not subsumed, on independence the convening party is the party under test, and on cost the record's one pre-registered measurement runs against multi-agent at thirty times the tokens. The survey's reading is that the two are not substitutes: the expert system dominates the function the author wanted more of and does not reach the function the review was constituted for, which is the division both experts of 2026-09-08 reached independently and why the author's mark reversed at `words/2026-09-08/38`.
