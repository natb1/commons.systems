## Answer

Patent prosecution is the tradition that has kept a complete record of rejected
alternatives for the longest, at the highest stakes, and before the most
adversarial readership there is. Every claim an applicant gave up in order to get
the patent stays on the public record, and that record is not inert: it estops. The
Court states the reason for keeping it in the register of notice — "competitors may
rely on the prosecution history, the public record of the patent proceedings" — and
states the reason it binds — "Were it otherwise, the inventor might avoid the PTO's
gatekeeping role and seek to recapture in an infringement action the very subject
matter surrendered as a condition of receiving the patent."

So this record's practice of keeping what was passed over, attributed, has a
two-century precedent under conditions harsher than anything here. That is the
support, and it is not thin: the tradition kept the alternatives because a reader
downstream needs to know what the author gave up, which is this record's own reason
almost word for word.

What the tradition adds, and what the recommendation has not yet answered, is the
cost side. A recorded position by a named party becomes a boundary that party is
held to and must argue their way out of. The Court rejected an absolute bar and
made the estoppel a rebuttable presumption instead, but the presumption runs
against the party whose record it is, and the Court was explicit about who carries
the weight of escaping it: "We hold here that the patentee should bear the burden
of showing that the amendment does not surrender the particular equivalent in
question."

The analogue here is exact and is not a stretch, because this record already reads
positions back. `deferring-on-a-probe` constructs the author's choice by reading it
out of what is recorded, and `what-acts-during-bootstrap` makes bootstrap
reconciliation act on the convergence of that constructed choice with the AI's
recommendation. A mark saying the author chose an option and did not confirm it, or
that a named expert chose it, is therefore a recorded position by a named party
that the machinery will later act on. Festo's finding is that such a mark becomes a
boundary, and its remedy is to say on the face of the doctrine who bears the burden
of getting out from behind one.

The recommendation mints those two marks and mints no corresponding instrument for
lifting either. The node's own text records that the record cannot presently encode
the first of them at all, `recording`'s ruling responses being `confirm` and `edit`,
neither of which writes a choice made and not confirmed. So on the recommendation as
it stands there is a mark with no producer and no retraction, on a row the machinery
reads. That is a gap in the recommendation and not an argument against it, and the
tradition names both the gap and the shape of the repair: a stated way for the
party who was marked to lift the mark, and a stated answer to who carries the
burden when they try.
