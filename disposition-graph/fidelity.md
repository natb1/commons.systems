## Account

Confirmation gates authority: it prevents an answer being stamped at a class the author did not grant, and `authority` makes the guard explicit, a ratified stamp whose ruling is not in the record being invalid. It gates nothing about meaning. A node can arrive at confirmation carrying the author's authority class correctly and the author's intention only distantly, and the confirmation ritual cannot tell the two cases apart, because by then the thing being confirmed is the AI's text.

### What the record already has, and what each protects

Four mechanisms bear on this, and all four protect the author's words as *text*:

1. `growth`'s periagogic conduct: the AI's account, findings, drafts, and recommendations are held back until the author has committed an answer to the probe, and enter only as counterpoint, cited to the text by locus. This protects the author's *fresh articulation* against anchoring, within one sitting.
2. `dialogue` and `checkpoint`: `## Disposition` carries the author's words verbatim and dated, accumulating, written the turn they are said and before anything is drafted from them. This protects the *token* — the words survive the session that heard them.
3. `frontier-consistency`: every disposition the author has given is answered by exactly one node, none unanswered and none answered twice. This protects *coverage* — that no words are dropped.
4. `quotes` and `recording`: the words are quoted into the rationale at the recording. This protects *provenance* — that a ruling can be traced.

Preserved token, coverage, provenance, and freshness. Not preserved: whether the answer still means what the author meant. Coverage asks whether every word was answered; nothing asks whether the answer is the author's intention or the AI's reconstruction of it. That is the gap this question names.

### The mechanism of the loss

The dialogue is additive and one-sided. At every movement the AI writes: a proposal, a review, a counter-argument, a reply, a frontier finding. The author's contribution is fixed at the moment of speaking and never grows. So the node's centre of gravity moves away from the author monotonically, and the frame in which the question is read becomes the AI's, not because any step was wrong but because every step was the AI's.

Measured on this graph on 2026-09-03, over the 43 nodes carrying the author's words, comparing bytes of `## Disposition` against bytes of `## Proposal`:

Across the whole graph: 26,088 bytes of the author's words against 396,323 bytes of the AI's account, a signal share of 6.2%. The most-worked nodes are the quietest: `web-routing` 1.9%, `capture` 2.0%, `knowledge-store` 2.3%, `audience` 2.8%, `dialogue` 2.9%, `transience` 3.1%.

The shape matters more than the level. The drop is not gradual — it happens at the second review round, from about 13% to about 4%, and never recovers. The dialogue's own quality machinery is what dilutes the signal, and it does most of its diluting exactly when a node is being prepared for the author's ruling.

Byte ratio is a proxy and a crude one: a short disposition can be fully honoured by a long account, and a high ratio can accompany a bad reading. It is offered as a symptom that something is unmeasured, not as the measure itself. Naming the right measure is part of what this question asks.

### Evidence from the session that raised it

- `lockfile`: the author's 40 words of disposition sit in a node whose account runs to roughly seven hundred, written in the same turn.
- `un-aligned-children`: the AI recorded the author's challenge inside a frame the author had not accepted, presenting a rule as binding that the record made overrulable. The author's next words corrected the frame. Coverage was perfect and the intention was misread.
- Twice in the same session the AI called deferred answers "doctrine", which `authority` reserves for ratified answers and of which this graph has none. The author corrected it. Vocabulary the record defines drifted inside the session that was rationalizing the record.

None of these would have been caught at confirmation, and two were caught only because the author was present and reading closely. That is the failure mode: the guard is the author's attention, which is the scarce thing the whole record exists to conserve.
