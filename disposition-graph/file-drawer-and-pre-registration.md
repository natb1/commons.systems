## Answer

Supports keeping the candidate that was tried and failed, and names the harm precisely enough to be useful. Rosenthal's problem is not that failed work is uninteresting; it is that its absence is itself a distortion. A reader of the published literature cannot tell a question nobody asked from a question asked and answered in the negative, so the same study is run again, and the estimate the record supports is the estimate of the subset that survived a filter nobody can see. Pre-registration answers it by moving the record earlier than the outcome, so what is on file is what was attempted, whatever it found.

The record takes it for the option list. A candidate the AI holds dominated keeps its row on the fact with the status passed and the one clause saying why, so a later session meets both the candidate and the judgment against it. The reason the author gave for persisting options is regression, and this tradition is why that reason bears at least as hard on the rejected candidate as on the surviving one: the next session is at least as likely to re-propose what was tried and failed, and it will re-propose it precisely because nothing tells it the attempt was made. Version control is not a projection, so a candidate that leaves the record is met by nobody, which is the file drawer with a commit hash on it.

The shelving is part of the tradition and the reading should say so, since this record's rule is to surface what pre-agent constraints put away. Publishing null results was unaffordable for as long as a journal was printed pages that cost money and readers' attention was the scarce thing; the practice arrived when the constraint went. Here the constraint is the length of the page the author reads, which the recommendation's own case against prices at a hundred and four rows, and the record's answer is the mark rather than the deletion.

Where the tradition and the record part, with the counter attached. Pre-registration binds before the outcome is known, and that is the whole of its force: the commitment is made by a party who cannot yet know which way it will cut. The record's status is written after the judgment, by the party whose judgment it is, on its own authority, and lifted on the same authority. What the record has instead is that the mark can be argued with, since the author may rule for a passed-over option and only the author strikes; that is a check on the mark and not the prior commitment the tradition asks for, and the tradition would not accept it as one.
