## Answer

The engineering lessons-learned register is the control case for retention with no
presentation discipline, and its verdict on itself is blunt: "The need to learn from
one project to the next is clearly of vital importance, but is often neglected.
Furthermore, there are fundamental issues within projects that inhibit such
learning." The register exists, entries accumulate, compliance is enforced, and the
decision that would have used an entry is made without opening it. The literature's
steady finding is that the failure is not sparseness but non-consultation.

What killed these registers was not that a record was too dense. It was that the
record was not delivered at the point of decision, sitting instead in a separate
store the decider had to choose to open. That is where the divergence lands, and it
is why this reading diverges mildly rather than opposing the recommendation: the
recommendation's risk is not the marks it puts on the row, it is what the row's fold
is left holding.

Read against this record as it presently stands, the shape is the register's in
miniature. Two of the marks have no producer, so a row shows the marks that can be
produced plus two structural absences at the top, while the AI's written objection,
where one exists at all, sits behind the fold. A row that is dense where nothing is
contested and hollow where the argument actually is reproduces the register's
failure at the scale of a single line: full at the surface the reader meets, empty
at the surface the reader would have to seek.

On absences that look like defects, the traditions offer no measured answer and this
reading invents none. What they offer instead is a procedure, and it comes from
`eduyot-minority-opinion`: the analogous problem there, a reader taking a retained
item for something it is not, is handled by adding a mark that names the item's
standing and never by omitting the item. The transposition is direct. Where a mark
is structurally absent because no producer exists, an absence that is silent reads
as an absence that was judged, and the tradition's move is to say on the row which
one it is.
