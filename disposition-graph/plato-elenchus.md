Yes, for the operation the other two movements turn on. Refutation in Plato serves three functions the record must keep apart, because it has different instruments for them and they are not equally at risk of capture.

The first is purgative and comes before anything else. In the Sophist cross-examination is the greatest of purifications: the beliefs are set against one another, the conflict is shown, and what is purged is the conceit of knowing, without which instruction does not take, as a body takes no nourishment while the obstruction stands. Read on this record, a disposition drawn from an author or drafted by an AI that has not been refuted is a well-formed record of a conceit, and a review placed only at the end of a sitting arrives after the moment refutation was for.

The second is the wind egg test. In the Theaetetus the midwife's greatest skill is not delivery but judging whether what is delivered is a phantom or something true, and the test belongs to whoever did the delivering. On this record that is the sitting's own duty toward what it has just drawn out, and it is what the evaluation node means in saying that adversarial review of one's own recorded output is part of producing it.

The third is about the refuter.
