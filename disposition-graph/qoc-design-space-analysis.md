```markdown
## Answer

The tradition that invented this object, and the closest structural precedent the
record has for a row carrying several named parties' assessments and no tally. A
Question opens a decision; the Options are the alternative answers to it, all
retained, chosen and rejected alike; the Criteria are the reasons; and each Option
is joined to each Criterion by an assessment that is positive or negative. The
chosen Option is distinguished inside the space rather than lifted out of it, so
what the record keeps is the reasoning and not the outcome. The assessments are
deliberately not summed: the notation records one link per criterion and refuses
to produce a score, on the ground that the weighing is the designer's act and not
the notation's.

The record takes two things. The first is the refusal to aggregate, which is what
`five-marks-and-the-two-the-author-added` writes as one mark per expert and no
tally, and the author's own reason for it — that the AI's recommendation
integrates expert choice without requiring expert consensus — is QOC's reason in
other words. The second is the separation of what a record retains from how a
record is drawn, which this tradition draws in its bones: the 1997 successor is an
empirical study of the graphical rendering of QOC with the design space held
constant, and a tradition that runs experiments on how to draw its records without
reopening what they contain has already made the distinction the record needs
here.

The divergence is the tradition's own unresolved problem, and it is the case
against the option this reading supports. The design space grows faster than any
reader's willingness to walk it, and what QOC never solved is that a fully
assessed space becomes a picture nobody consults. Adding two marks to a row that
already carries three moves in that direction, and QOC had an advantage this
record does not: its marks were criteria, so a reader walking them was reading
reasons, where marks (1), (2) and (5) here are parties, and a reader walking them
is reading who chose.
```
