## Answer

Supports the audience's framing and marks the divergence that defines this repository. Adopted: the factory is real, and the aim of making software production a repeatable process is shared. Diverged: a factory optimises throughput through standardisation, and its literature is about the factory's own process. This repository is about the owner's intent, which the factory acts under. In Aristotle's image the factory is the self-working instrument; this record is what keeps the instrument's owner the origin of the work.

## Rationale

The Japanese factories of the 1970s and 1980s and the model-driven factories of the 2000s both answered "how do we produce software reliably at scale"; the agentic factories of 2025 answer it again with agents. None answers "how does the owner stay the origin of what the factory makes", which is the question above this one.
