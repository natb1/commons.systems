## Disposition

> The fronteir-survey appears to be very expensive. Is it part of the recommended alignment design from this sitting? What function does it serve that's not served by the frontier consistency check?

The author, 2026-09-07, in reply to the session's answer on what the survey serves and what it costs:

The author, 2026-09-07, while the design of the survey's optimization was in hand, on what shape it must take:

The author, 2026-09-07, in the next turn:

## Answer

By cuts taken in one order, each of which leaves behind the evidence of what it cost. `survey` here is the frontier survey the frontier-consistency node defines; what its validations read of a node is that node's and is cited and not restated, and what a reading costs is the review-cost node's.

**The judged node is carried once.** The judged set is the nodes a survey judges, and a judged node is carried by what the validations read of it: its question, the author's words its options carry, the one answer that binds it — the resolved content of the option last confirmed, or of the option the answer fact recommends where none is confirmed — and, of its answer fact, each option's name, its source, its ref and its status, with the sentence saying what that option would answer, which is what the fifteenth validation reads of an option. The prose of its facts, the AI's accumulated support and divergence on its options, and the resolved content of the options the fact does not recommend are not carried, because no validation from the seventh to the sixteenth reads them, and the review-cost node's rule is that a part no validation reaches is struck rather than shortened. It is never carried twice: the answer is the resolved content of one option and no structured render stands beside it.

**The mechanical tier gates the launch.** The mechanical tier is the set of checks the validator holds and runs over the whole graph before a brief is written, and no reader is launched while one of them reports a finding: a node that fails the tier is repaired or kicked back, and it is never read for it. The checks are an unresolved `depends`, `bears` or `under` reference; a recommendation past its pin; two options of one name on one fact; an option whose content resolves through a cycle, through a name no option carries, or through a hunk that does not apply exactly; a term used by a node with no path to the node whose `defines` carries it, read off a term concordance the projector emits; a reference to the author's words that resolves to no entry, read off the ledger; a passage byte-identical across nodes above a stated length; and an account section the unconfirmed-accumulation node's fold could have reached and has not. An entry of the author's words that no option anywhere references is reported beside the tier and gates nothing, since it may be context the record has not yet attached. The tier's bound is part of the rule: a check enters it only where it is obviously right when it fires, and anything merely probable enters the brief as a hint the reader may ignore and never as a gate, because a tier the applying session learns to disbelieve is worth less than no tier. A clean tier is not a clean frontier and is never reported as one: the reading's own report says which checks ran. This is the review-cost node's rule that a defect an instrument can name is the instrument's, carried to the point where it changes what happens: the debt is paid before the reader is, and not after.

**The selection, and what it gives up.** The judged set is every node at the review or the ruling stage whose recommendation has moved past its survey pin, together with every node whose read text differs from the hash the last survey wrote on it. A judged node is compared against the nodes that reach it, on the frontier-consistency node's reach, and against the other judged nodes. A pair both of whose members are unchanged since a survey read them together is not compared, and a node the judged set reaches but whose read text has not changed since that survey is carried on one line rather than by what it answers, because the pair has already been read once. The frozen set is everything so struck. It is named in the survey's own output and in the reader's report, so that what was not read is a fact of the run and not an inference from the generator. The selection is unsafe, and the answer says so: two backstops are part of the rule. A whole survey, in which nothing is frozen, runs on a stated cadence and unconditionally after any amendment to the validations, to what a reading is given, or to the tier, because a change in what the reader is asked to look for invalidates every earlier reading's silence. And every delta survey carries a drift probe: a small random sample of frozen pairs, handed to the reader like any other pair and marked as the probe, a finding anywhere in the sample forcing a whole survey.

**What the survey leaves on the node.** What a node carries between surveys is the dialogue node's question and, before a confirmation, the unconfirmed-accumulation node's; what this node needs of them is named, so the dependency is visible: the commit the survey read at, the hashes of the five sections its validations read, the register of the findings it left open on the node with the support each rests on and the condition on which each is discharged, and the keys the pairs touching that node were drawn on. The five sections are the question; the answer, being the resolved content of the option last confirmed or, where none is, of the option the answer fact recommends; the option names with their statuses and their sentences; the resolved content of every other option on the answer fact, since a rival's content is a draft the seventh and fifteenth validations read; and the author's words the options carry, resolved from their references in option order and date order, since the eighth and the fourteenth read the words. The delta compares hashes and not commits, so a change to the AI's accumulated support or divergence on an option, to an account, or to a fact's reason propagates nothing — which is also why the accumulation's fold and its absorptions, whose object is exactly those parts, move no hash and re-judge nothing. A finding whose support is unmoved is carried forward and reported as standing rather than as new; a finding one of whose supports moved is re-derived; a finding not in the register is new.

**The candidate pairs.** A candidate pair is two nodes the generator nominates for comparison, with the key that nominated it. The keys are the union of several cheap ones — a defined term shared, an entry of the author's words referenced by options on both, a citation either way in prose or in `depends`, a shared parent, and near-duplicate resemblance above a stated threshold — and each pair is handed to the reader with its key, so the reader is told where to look and what to look for. The key is recorded with any finding it produced, so that a key's yield is measurable across surveys and one that has produced nothing may be demoted on the evidence. A key narrows attention and never the corpus: every node the brief carries stays readable, the pair list orders the reading rather than partitioning it, and a finding on a pair no key nominated is a finding like any other and is the one worth most, being the measurement of what the keys miss.

**What this does not decide.** How many readers the survey uses is not divided here: it is one reader, as the clean-context-review node has it. Which validations a survey runs is the frontier-consistency node's; what a reading costs and what a brief must fit are the review-cost node's; when a survey runs and what it gates are the clean-context-review node's and the recording node's; what a node carries is the dialogue node's and the unconfirmed-accumulation node's.

## Rationale

The recommendation is the weighing: the four options beneath it are one ladder, each the one above plus a clause, and each clause is judged on the support and divergence recorded under it in `## Facts`. Carrying the judged node once has support and no divergence, since it denies no validation an input; the tier has support in the measured share of mechanical findings and divergence in the refusal it adds; the delta is the first rung whose divergence is a real loss, a pair no reader looks at; the keys are what make that loss measurable. The fourth rung is where the design becomes self-correcting — the frozen set is visible, the probe measures the selection, the keys measure themselves — and a narrowing whose loss is not measured is one the record cannot rule on later.

Four traditions are read under this node and bear on its options: `fagan-entry-criteria` (adopted, on the tier), `verifying-traces-and-early-cutoff` (adopted, on the delta), `regression-test-selection` (diverged, on the delta, its safety guarantee resting on a sound dependency relation this record has none of), and `blocking-and-canopies` (adopted as nomination and diverged as partition, on the pairs). Nineteen further traditions were surfaced in the tradition survey of 2026-09-07 and are owed rather than read; the four bearing hardest on what is not adopted are tolerated inconsistency, which supplies the finding register's discharge condition; lint's economics and Coverity's false-positive threshold, which supply the tier's bound; MapReduce, whose cross-shard blindness is why the survey is not partitioned; and acceptance sampling with Deming's objection, the case against the drift probe.

Two designs the record has refused are answered here and not repeated. `review-cost#budget-per-sitting` stops the reading by the clock and stops whatever stood last in the queue; nothing here is a budget, since the delta stops on what nothing has touched since a reader read it, and the survey runs to the end of what it selects. `frontier-consistency#validator-rule-for-consistency` would ask a validator to decide whether two answers disagree; the tier holds only what is decidable from the schema and from text as bytes, and every judgment stays with the reader.

#### the-judged-node-is-carried-once

The judged node is carried by what the validations read of it and once only, its rationale, facts prose and option prose struck and its content never rendered twice: the answer's first two paragraphs and its last, and nothing between.

**AI support.** Measured on the survey brief of 2026-09-07 at graph commit `e4c87ed0`, 1,188,648 bytes for twenty-five judged nodes: the judged set is 799,187 bytes, of which the facts prose is 375,242 and the recommendation fences 208,479 — 73.6% of the judged text — and a judged node whose recommendation differs from what stands is rendered twice inside its own block, once as fields and once as the raw fence. Nothing from the seventh validation to the sixteenth reads a rationale, a fact's prose or an option's prose. It is the parent's own one-answer rule turned on the node being judged, and it is the whole of the saving that gives nothing up.

**AI divergence.** None recorded. A reader shown one text cannot see how far the node moved without opening the file, which is a cost the parent already carries for every node a reading is not judging and is not new here.

#### the-mechanical-tier-gates-the-launch

Everything above, with a mechanical tier the survey's reader is not launched over until it is clean: the answer's paragraph "**The mechanical tier gates the launch.**" added to `the-judged-node-is-carried-once`.

**AI support.** Of the thirteen findings the survey of 2026-09-07 returned, one was mechanical outright and nine partly so; of the seven distinct findings of 2026-09-05, three were mechanical, one of them a byte-identical sentence on forty-six nodes. The `review-cost` node already holds that such a defect is the instrument's and that every one the record has met is a check the validator is owed; a rule that says so and then launches the most expensive reader the record has over a graph that still holds them has said the thing and not done it.

**AI divergence.** It adds a refusal to write a brief at all, so a check that is wrong stops work the author asked for, and the cure is to fix the check, which is slower than ignoring a finding. A clean tier is also an affirmative signal, and a tier the AI sizes can make the record look audited exactly where it stopped auditing, which is why the reading's report must say which checks ran.

#### the-delta-survey-with-a-periodic-whole

Everything above, with the selection, what is judged, what is compared and what is frozen, and what the survey leaves on the node: the answer's paragraphs "**The selection, and what it gives up.**" and "**What the survey leaves on the node.**" added to `the-mechanical-tier-gates-the-launch`.

**AI support.** A survey that reads the graph entire prices every run at the frontier's whole size rather than at what moved, so the reader spends its attention re-deriving on every run the consistency an earlier reader established and pinned. Measured at graph commit `e02655c6`: 42 of 143 nodes had changed in what the survey reads since the last survey, so the delta's object is under a third of the whole on a frontier of this shape.

**AI divergence.** The selection is unsafe in regression selection's exact sense: safety requires a sound dependency relation and this record has none for the thing the survey exists to catch, since two nodes may contradict each other with no ancestry between them, no citation either way and no word in common. The drift probe is a check on the model and not on the record, and its power is only what its size gives it; Deming's objection to sampling, that where an escape is expensive you inspect everything or nothing, is the case against it and is why the whole survey stays in the design rather than being replaced by it.

#### several-readers-over-a-partition

Everything the recommendation says, with the survey divided across several readers over a partition of the judged set, each emitting structured claims with verbatim loci and a combining reader running the pairwise validations over the emitted keys; the clause in "**What this does not decide.**" that it is one reader is struck.

**AI support.** The brief of 2026-09-07 is 1,188,648 bytes and no reader holds it whole, which is the condition the parent calls a defect of the brief; this is the only option on the fact that answers it directly rather than by making the brief smaller and hoping.

**AI divergence.** Validations thirteen to sixteen are aggregate over the whole corpus and do not partition — coverage's input is an absence, and an absence has no shard. Splitting buys context capacity and not tokens, each reader re-reading the shared context, so cost per byte rises with the number of readers. And the record has decided against it from the other side twice, `clean-context-review#one-context-for-a-batch` because the survey keeps one context for the frontier's own object, and `main-thread-performs-the-surveys` because the size of a survey is unknown until it is read. With the accumulation beside it the recommended option brings the brief back inside one reader, which is the condition on which this stays unadopted; where it does not, this is the option to take.

#### a-whole-graph-reading-every-time

Every survey reads the graph entire, every judged node whole, every pair live, nothing frozen and no tier: the design as it runs today. The judged set is every node at the review or the ruling stage whose recommendation has moved past its survey pin, each is carried whole, each is compared against every other node in the record, and what bounds a survey is the review-cost node's rule on what a brief must fit and nothing else.

**AI support.** It is the only option under which the frozen set is empty and no finding is lost to selection, and it survives inside the recommended answer as the whole survey run on a cadence.

**AI divergence.** It prices every survey at the frontier's whole size rather than at what moved, so the cheapest survey the record can run is the one after a survey that changed nothing.

### authority

Ratified, on the capture-shaped limb of `class-recommendation`'s test and on that limb alone. This node decides how much of the record the survey is shown, and the survey is the only reader whose object is the AI's whole frontier judged against itself; the party that would set the answer is the party the answer checks. Two features make it the sharp case. A narrowing is invisible in the record's own terms — a survey that reads less returns fewer findings, and fewer findings on a frontier are indistinguishable from a cleaner frontier — so being wrong here leaves no trace the author could read. And a clean mechanical tier is an affirmative signal that something was checked, so a tier the AI sizes can make the record look audited exactly where it stopped auditing. The parent ruled the identical limb on the identical ground, that a rule letting the drafter narrow the review's object is capture-shaped and that a review reading too little looks cheaper and no worse, and this node is that rule one step narrower, so a delegation here would reach past a ratification there. Not irreversible: nothing here deletes, swaps or lands; the gate opens, the selection widens, and a design changed back at the next sitting leaves the graph as it was — which is where this node and the accumulation node part, since a fold does not. Not expensive, or weakly so: a wrong selection is paid in a survey that missed something, recovered by the whole survey this answer schedules. Boldness low: the test is stated and the limb is the parent's own.
