## Answer

What the alignment dialogue and its artifact read, and nothing else; the rest is folded into the history or absorbed into the parts they do read. The accumulation is that reduction, applied to a node still in dialogue; the fold is what it strikes; a manifest line is what stands in the place of what it struck.

**What the node carries.** What the dialogue node's encoding carries, cited and not restated, this answer adding only the test and the closure: its question and every frontmatter key that is its identity or an edge a projection walks, `form`, `under`, `depends`, `defines`, `cites`, `order`, `boost`, `tier`, its shims and its instrument, and its `probes`. Every fact, with the option it recommends, the boldness of that recommendation, the strongest case against it, and the fact's own reason, which is the judgment that weighs the support and the divergence across the options. Every option of every fact, with its name, its source and ref, its status and the reason it was passed over, its ruling where the author has given one, the references to the author's words that support it and the references to those it diverges from, the AI's accumulated support and its accumulated divergence kept whatever the fact recommends, and, on the answer fact, its content, whole or as a named change to another option's. The `stage`. The `review`, the draft reading's keys and the survey's block. The last reading's section of `## Account` and every section after it, which is what a re-reading is given, and the manifest lines. Nothing here is stored that can be derived: the confirmed label is not, the class is not, the tradition's support and divergence on an option is not, being the derived inverse of what the readings bear on, and the node's answer is not, being the resolved content of the option last confirmed.

**What is folded, and what is absorbed.** A section is absorbed where its content is material the dialogue needs in a place the dialogue does not read, and it is moved there rather than struck: the node-level `## Rationale`, whose argument goes to the recommended option's support, to the rivals' divergence and to the fact's own reason, and whose traditions go to the readings that bear on the options they inform; `## Disposition`, whose entries go to the ledger and reach the options as references, retained on an option only where they support it or it diverges from them; and an account section whose findings are now an option's divergence. A section is folded where its content is the dialogue's history and nothing reads it twice: every `## Account` section before the last clean-context reading's section, since that reading's findings and the session's replies to them are what a re-reading is given, as the review-cost node says; and a superseded `review` block. Nothing else. The fold has a list and does not have a size.

**The manifest line.** One line stands for each section folded or absorbed, under `## Account` and in the order the sections had. It names the section's kind and its date, and then, where the section was folded, the graph commit at which its full text stands, and where it was absorbed, the option, the fact or the ledger entry its content went to. A manifest line naming a commit that does not contain the text it names is a defect of the instrument and a finding of the validator.

**When it runs, and the condition on it.** At every checkpoint that lands, in the checkpoint node's sense, a transition of a node's stage, over the nodes that checkpoint lands, by an instrument and never at a session's choosing, since a set chosen by the session is a set with no trace of what it left out. It is idempotent. And it refuses to fold any text not already reachable from `origin/disposition`: the full history is in git because it is in git first, and an instrument that struck unpushed text would not be moving it to the history but ending it. Absorption is a separate pass and never runs unattended, because a rewrite is not a deletion and cannot be undone by fetching the old bytes; it is under the same condition, absorbing only text already reachable from `origin/disposition`, and its manifest line names both the commit at which the section stood and the option, fact or ledger entry it went to, so that the rewrite has an address back into the history as the fold does. The absorption of the author's words into the ledger moves the one hash of the survey-selection node's five that the words are, once, at the migration, and the survey after it is a whole one; every other fold and absorption moves no hash.

**What reads an accumulated node.** The alignment page, which renders the facts, the options with their three accumulations, and previews the node under the option the author selects — all of which the node keeps, which is the test this answer is drawn from. The browser. The two readings' briefs, which carry a node by what it answers or by what its validations read, and which on an accumulated node describe the file rather than filtering it. And a session resuming a node, which meets every option, every word of the author's for and against it and the case both ways, and reaches the argument behind anything folded through the commit its manifest line names.

**What is not decided here.** How an option's content, its references and its accumulated support and divergence are encoded is the dialogue node's, whose answer this one uses and does not restate. Where the author's words are kept is the quotes node's. What a node carries at and after the confirmation is the dialogue node's and the recording node's. What a reading is given, and what a survey selects, are the review-cost and survey-selection nodes'. Nothing this answer names is materialized on 2026-09-07: no instrument folds, no manifest line exists, and the validator checks none; the instrument, `accumulate.mjs`, is the reconciliation the author's grant of that day covers.
