## Disposition

The author, 2026-09-02:

## Proposal

What stands today: the `defines` field names the terms a node defines ("disposition" by model, "ratified" and "doctrine" by authority, "archē" by public/agency); the browser links every occurrence of a defined term to the node that defines it and renders readings as a block set apart, with source, locus, and relation; the vocabulary page the first browser carried was removed at the author's direction on 2026-09-03, because the browser states nothing of its own. Open for the sitting: whether the term links and the readings block satisfy the disposition, or a vocabulary page projected from the `defines` fields is owed, and where on the onboarding path vocabulary is met. The periagogic object is the projection page and the browser as published.
