  The obligation-shaped counterpart to kind-virtue for mounted content. When the
  graph mounts a social relationship — a family bond, a client or employer
  engagement — the counterparty's model is not always a disposition the author
  holds; often it is a specific obligation OWED across the mount boundary. A
  duty is discharged (met on its terms, for a party, at a time), not
  held-and-exercised the way a virtue is: that is the virtue/duty line. A
  mounted duty carries its own internal `parent`/`serves` structure (the modeled
  graph's shape) and stays out of this graph's attention flow, exactly as every
  mounted node does.

  (duties carry no attention injection) and is NOT a mount anchor (a duty is
  mounted content, never the record a mount anchors on — that is kind-delegation
  and kind-tradition). Ratified at tactic-mount-owner-review.
