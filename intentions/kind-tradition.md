rationale: >-
  The answer to 'is a philosophy a delegation?' is: a sibling record kind, not a
  reuse. kind-delegation's divergence axis applies to a tradition in full —
  adopting a philosophy grafts its vocabulary and problem-framings onto this
  graph, and imported language reshaping how problems are perceived is exactly
  the capture mechanism virtue-philosophical-mobility names. But nothing is
  handed over and there is no live counterparty, so delegation's irreversibility
  fields (recovery_path, gated, last_exercised) would be dead weight on a
  text-based tradition. A tradition record therefore keeps the audit spirit and
  drops the service fields: it documents what is adopted (with its graph locus),
  what is diverged from (with why), where this tradition is chosen over a
  sibling, and its origin — chosen, inherited (absorbed through culture before
  it was examined), or declined (deliberately refused; the refusal kept
  auditable instead of tacit, extending kind-delegation's declined doctrine).


  The capture surface of a tradition: it captures not by charging for a service
  but by becoming the only vocabulary in which problems can be stated. The
  standing audit is whether the graph can still state its commitments in more
  than one tradition's terms. A record whose diverged list is empty is a warning
  sign, not an achievement.


  Traditions are records, not goals: reviewed on their review_trigger, never
  completed. They carry no attention and are never conduits for rank flow.
  Virtue and strategy nodes point at them via attributes.traditions; the
  alignment detail lives here, on the tradition record, so cross-tradition
  choices ('Kant over Aristotle for the floor') have one auditable home.
  Recorded 2026-07-04 interview (the first strategy-philosophical-grounding
  round).
