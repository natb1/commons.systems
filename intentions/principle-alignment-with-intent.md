  carried structurally, by absence: there is no server, account, or data to
  mine, and the human is retained in planning and design. A platform inserts

  a ratchet that converts a required institution into a parasitic one. An owned
  workflow has no such surface; intent stays where it was set. The advantage is
  in what is missing, not in any charge against incumbents, and it is held to
  the same tier-signal discipline as the orchestration layer above — never
  validated by assertion."
