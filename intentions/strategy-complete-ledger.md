rationale: >-
  virtue-alignment-of-attachments calls detection the daily exercise, but
  detection reads the delegation records — and nothing owned their completeness.
  The portfolio review (strategy-exercise-recovery-paths) reviews the records
  that exist; an attachment with no record is invisible to it, to the dialectic,
  and to the discernment virtue-philosophical-mobility says must never atrophy.
  The leak was real, not hypothetical: strategy-durable-owned-data had to call
  its own Drive dependency an unaudited attachment, strategy-recover-finance's
  bank condition read against no record, and the phone — plausibly the largest
  live surface — had no record at all until the 2026-07-02 sweep added
  delegation-mobile-platform, delegation-banking, delegation-connectivity, and
  delegation-cloud-backup.


  This strategy owns two things. The intake practice: an attachment entering
  household life gets a delegation record within a review cycle of adoption —
  recorded first, assessed after; a raw record with first-pass axes beats an
  invisible edge. And the standing sweep: each portfolio review closes with the
  completeness question — what does the household now depend on that carries no
  record? Scope follows the virtues as clarified 2026-07-02: every institutional
  attachment, digital or not — utilities, insurance, transport, food supply
  enter the ledger as they become material.
