rationale: >-
  virtue-philosophical-mobility cannot fully verify itself — capture corrupts
  the sensor that detects capture — so its calibration must be external. Today
  the named calibration mechanism, adversarial review, runs on the audited
  vendor's own models: the align contrarian pass executes on the very delegation
  (delegation-anthropic-claude) it would have to catch. This strategy exists to
  close that hole; it is the only strategy whose direct object is the graph's
  one non-delegable virtue.


  The chosen mechanism is the practitioner community — nearer term, the existing
  communities strategy-join-existing-practice participates in; eventually the
  one strategy-promote-progressive-detachment cultivates — not cross-vendor
  model review, not named peers, for now. The division of labor: promote
  supplies the community; this strategy owns the calibration function —
  soliciting and registering challenges from people with no stake in this
  graph's delegations, and letting a challenge actually move a priority.
  Challenges extend beyond priorities to a node's framing and to the
  other-directed methods virtue-respect-for-persons governs — an outside report
  that an artifact locks people in is calibration too, which is why this
  strategy serves that root as well.


  Stated honestly, this is currently a gap. The practitioner tier (tier 3) is
  unvalidated and no external calibration event has yet occurred; the signal
  below presently reads zero. Recording that plainly is the point — a
  self-audited "I am not captured" is exactly what a captured agent reports.
