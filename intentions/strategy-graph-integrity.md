  - question: What does 'closed, fully justified by defined virtues, or delegated to
      mounted graphs' mean operationally?
    answer: "A trichotomy every node must satisfy at least one limb of. Closed:
      completed or retired content leaves the graph — tactics prune on done,
      strategies leave by condition-expiry or deliberate retirement
      (kind-strategy lifecycle). Justified: the node's motivation chain (serves
      plus parent inheritance) terminates in a defined virtue root, and the
      content actually expresses that virtue — not merely that the edge
      resolves. Delegated: the node's motivation crosses a recorded delegation
      or mount boundary — delegation records today, the distinct graft relation
      once strategy-graph-mounts lands. Recorded 2026-07-09 interview."

  - question: How do prose references to node ids stay consistent with a graph whose
      tactics prune on completion?
    answer: "A three-class convention. Live: a reference to a node expected to exist
      must resolve. Planned: a forward reference is legal while a queued tactic
      owns creating the target (the reading chunks' tradition-* records,
      delegation-food-supply, delegation-tailscale). Pruned: a reference to a
      completed, pruned tactic is legal history — the digest lists pruned
      references, and new writing prefers naming the merged PR or date alongside
      the id; no mass rewrite of existing prose. A reference to a target that
      was never created and is owned by no queued tactic is a violation; none
      exist as of this round. Recorded 2026-07-09 interview."
