statement: Manage shared attachments with household consent — the practice is
  offered, never imposed
owner: human
status: refining
parent: null
rationale: >-
  virtue-respect-for-persons currently generates only project-outward
  strategies, but the nearest persons are the household: family members share
  attachments — photos, calendars, messaging, media — and every recovery or
  re-alignment move against a shared delegation changes their lives too. This
  strategy applies the virtue inward. Migration choices on shared attachments
  are made with the household, not for it; consent is sought before the move,
  not announced after it. A family member's platform preference is respected
  as their agency even when it keeps a household attachment alive — respect
  forbids conscripting family into the practice exactly as it forbids
  converting users. And recovery paths for shared data must preserve the
  household's access on their terms, not just the author's: an export only
  the author can read is not a recovered family archive — the standing
  durability half of that requirement is owned by strategy-durable-owned-data.


  This bounds the sibling strategies: strategy-realign-attachments and the
  recover-* family treat a shared delegation as jointly owned — the household
  holds a veto the portfolio review records rather than routes around.
