rationale: >-
  The second mode of exercising detachment: help others recover autonomy.
  Promotion is self-interested at one remove — every practitioner added
  strengthens the non-captured substrate my own recovery paths bottom out in,
  and a community of practitioners is the external calibration my own
  discernment needs. That is why this strategy also serves
  virtue-philosophical-mobility: it supplies the community that
  strategy-external-calibration draws its calibration function from. It is also
  intrinsically required by virtue-respect-for-persons, which alone dictates its
  methods: demonstration over argument, selection over conversion, capability
  transfer over dependency.


  Pacing is governed by strategy-progressive-validation: each audience tier
  (users, practitioners, collaborators) adds ratchet teeth — documentation
  obligations, conventions, legitimacy, cultural identity — and teeth are
  accepted only when the previous tier validates the need.

clarifications:
  - question: What does the privilege gradient do to this strategy's validation, and
      what is the mitigation?
    answer: "The practice requires surplus on three axes — money (DRM-free
      purchases, self-hosting, owned hardware, runway), time (office-hours
      sittings, reading curricula, maintaining forks), and technical skill. The
      author holds all three; most people hold none. Consequences, carried from
      the former CHARTER.md: the tier signals validate against people with the
      same surplus, so adoption-with-migration-freedom among privileged
      technologists can read green while the practice stays structurally
      inaccessible — a validation distortion the signals cannot detect by
      themselves; and left unstated, the gradient lets the project drift into
      lifestyle content for well-off technologists. The mitigation is the
      translation bet: the practice reaches beyond the surplus tier only through
      translators — journalists, educators, policy advocates — who convert
      demonstrated practice into forms usable without the surplus. The author
      bets on being translatable (honest artifacts, documented reasoning,
      share-alike license), not on doing the translating; tier-3 practitioners
      was never meant to mean only self-hosters, and a translator engaging
      counts toward the tier-3 signal. Recorded 2026-07-08 interview."
  - question: Is the practice more than lifestyle optimization for technical
      professionals?
    answer: "Held open, deliberately. The honest test — value delivered beyond the
      surplus tier — cannot fire while the tier gate holds: with tiers 2 and 3
      undeclared, absent broader adoption is indistinguishable from
      not-yet-inviting, so any signal recorded today would be a fake proxy.
      Self-report is also structurally unreliable here — this record's author is
      the person the lifestyle reading flatters, the same shape as
      virtue-philosophical-mobility's caveat that a self-audited
      I-am-not-captured is exactly what a captured agent reports. Resolution is
      therefore deferred to external calibration once the outward tiers open,
      with the translation bet the main path by which a wider-than-lifestyle
      claim could ever be earned. Until then the claims discipline on
      strategy-show-not-tell bounds what may be asserted. Recorded 2026-07-08
      interview."
