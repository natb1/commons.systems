  - question: How is deferral severity measured when ordering recovery targets?
    answer: "Axes first, load as tie-breaker. Primary: the delegation record's own
      audit axes — classification (captured > platform > tool), then divergence
      level. Tie-breaker: load — how much daily life or graph doctrine flows
      through the deferral, which is what separates attention-services from
      social-publishing when both read captured/high-divergence. Curriculum

      doctrine load, ratifying the reading program's original ordering
      principle. Recorded 2026-07-06 interview."
  - question: Does 'foundational work first' mean breadth-first foundations?
    answer: "No — critical-path discipline only. A foundation is done first only
      when it sits on the dependency path to the current highest-severity
      target; foundational work on no path to any target waits, however virtuous
      it feels. This is what distinguishes a critical path from a curriculum
      sweep, and it is the clause with bite: it forbids comfortable low-stakes
      foundation-laying while the deepest captures stand open. Recorded
      2026-07-06 interview."
  - question: Why does a strategy for resolving capture carry no recovers edge?
    answer: "Sequencing is not unwinding (kind-delegation's abstention
      doctrine, 2026-07-09, is the auditable home: a strategy that is not
      unwinding an entered delegation is not a recovers target) — the precedent
      is strategy-realign-attachments, which abstains for the same reason. The
      recovers edges stay on the strategies performing the recovery:
      strategy-recover-attention, strategy-recover-finance,
      strategy-own-audience and strategy-recover-publishing,
      strategy-philosophical-grounding. An edge here would satisfy 'every
      high-divergence record is covered' portfolio checks while no work in this
      node unwinds anything — exactly the false-coverage signal
      realign-attachments' threshold guards against. Recorded 2026-07-06
      interview."
  - question: Division of labor with the neighboring recovery-governance strategies?
    answer: strategy-domain-selection owns admission (which domains get a recovery
      strategy at all), strategy-exercise-recovery-paths owns coverage (every
      path exercised, the portfolio review cadence),
      strategy-realign-attachments owns the cheap divergence swap,
      strategy-philosophical-grounding owns the tradition dialectic. This
      strategy owns nothing but the order they are worked in; the severity
      inputs it reads are maintained by exercise-recovery-paths' portfolio
      review. Recorded 2026-07-06 interview.
  - question: Do context chunks violate the foundations clause while the deepest
      captures stand open?
    answer: "No — context reading is capture work, not comfortable
      foundation-laying: a context chunk audits the delegatee's excerpt
      selection for the record it deepens (selection bias is a
      borrowed-articulation capture surface, observed live in the 2026-07-07
      chunk-1 session). But it never preempts focused reading: reading priority
      is ordered by distance from a critical-path node — focused chunks (verify

      unrestricted at any time; the gate is on reading priority, not on what may
      enter the backlog (that admission is strategy-complete-grounding's).
      Recorded 2026-07-08 interview."
