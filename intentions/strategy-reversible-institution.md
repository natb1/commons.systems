rationale: >-
  The reflexive application of virtue-philosophical-mobility: this project is
  itself an institution, and it may ratchet up when that serves its purpose —
  growth is not the pathology; the inability to reverse it is. The virtue's own
  terms: the project must stay organized so that standing down is what happens
  by inaction, never an act of will that a ratcheted institution never performs.


  This strategy owns the inventory of the project's ratchet teeth —
  documentation obligations, conventions, the dispatch chain, the intention
  graph itself, legitimacy, funding — and the standing verification that each
  still degrades by inaction: when demand or the author's need stops,
  coordination stops, and the project decays to referrals and archives without
  anyone deciding to kill it. Its children govern the two sharpest teeth:
  strategy-progressive-validation paces when new teeth may be accepted at all,
  and strategy-financial-sustainability keeps the most dangerous tooth — funding
  — reversible.


  The general definition of ratcheting — teeth individually reasonable to add,
  individually costly to remove, so removal never happens by will — lives
  canonically on virtue-philosophical-mobility; this strategy is its reflexive
  application. The repo-wide CC-BY-SA share-alike license (recorded on
  strategy-open-source-as-gift) is a stand-down precondition: archives remain
  legally usable and forkable with no live licensor, so decay to referrals and
  archives strands no one.
