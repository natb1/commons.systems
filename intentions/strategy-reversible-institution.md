rationale: >-
  The reflexive application of virtue-philosophical-mobility: this project
  is itself an institution, and it may ratchet up when that serves its
  purpose — growth is not the pathology; the inability to reverse it is.
  The virtue's own terms: the project must stay organized so that standing
  down is what happens by inaction, never an act of will that a ratcheted
  institution never performs.


  This strategy owns the inventory of the project's ratchet teeth —
  documentation obligations, conventions, the dispatch chain, legitimacy,
  funding — and the standing verification that each still degrades by
  inaction: when demand or the author's need stops, coordination stops, and
  the project decays to referrals and archives without anyone deciding to
  kill it. Its children govern the two sharpest teeth:
  strategy-progressive-validation paces when new teeth may be accepted at
  all, and strategy-financial-sustainability keeps the most dangerous tooth
  — funding — reversible.
