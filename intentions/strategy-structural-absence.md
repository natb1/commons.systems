statement: Carry alignment with intent structurally, by absence
owner: human
status: codified
parent: strategy-owned-orchestration
serves:
  - virtue-alignment-of-attachments
  - virtue-respect-for-persons
rationale: >-
  Who sets the problem stays with the practitioner — and when the
  practitioner is someone else, that same absence is
  virtue-respect-for-persons at work: nothing in the artifact can annex
  their agency because the annexing surface does not exist. It is carried
  structurally, by absence: there is no server, account, or data to mine, and
  the human is retained in planning and design. A platform inserts itself as
  the runtime and, over time, redefines the problem to fit its model — a
  ratchet that converts a required institution into a parasitic one. An owned
  workflow has no such surface; intent stays where it was set. The advantage
  is in what is missing, not in any charge against incumbents, and it is held
  to the same tier-signal discipline as the orchestration layer above — never
  validated by assertion.

  is_proxy: false
