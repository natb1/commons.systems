rationale: >-
  Engage with tabletop games — through the fellspiral blog and the local game
  group it links — as a subtle promotion of mobility. Tabletop play demonstrates
  recovery from media institutions in the most legible register there is: it
  requires only pencil, paper, and individual creative intention, and it
  promotes storytelling itself — a medium no institution gates. Nobody at the
  table needs the word "capture" to experience an evening of story that arrived
  without a platform, a feed, or a subscription.


  Per strategy-show-not-tell, the philosophy stays invisible in the content: the
  blog is a game blog and the table is a game table; no one is asked to hold a
  position on institutional capture. This is other-directed promotion, so it
  carries no recovers edge — but the institutions it routes around are the ones
  recorded at delegation-attention-services and delegation-media-libraries.
