# Encode curriculum maintenance into the /align interview: record-time enrollment of every recorded node — a deferral's mode-A re-validation item is added to the curriculum frontier, author-owned doctrine enrolls for mode-B confirmation reached by the frontier's recursive expansion

Retained draft from the 2026-07-09 /align-strategy review-curriculum round —
input to a future /align-tactics pass; not yet a plan. Decision record: the
/align-role clarification on strategy-graph-review-curriculum.

## Context

strategy-graph-review-curriculum makes maintaining the ever-expanding review
curriculum one of /align's roles, with enrollment at record time:

- **Deferrals (mode A)**: recording a deferral creates its re-validation
  review item — a reading chunk when a text grounds it, an office-hours
  sitting otherwise — and that item is a **curriculum frontier entry**. This
  is the universal deferral rule's existing mechanism, restated as frontier
  addition; the deferral-mechanics clause of
  tactic-align-interview-type-doctrine (same SKILL.md surface) carries the
  encoding of that half — coordinate, do not duplicate.
- **Author-owned recordings (mode B)**: recording author-owned doctrine
  enrolls it for confirmation review, reached periodically as the frontier's
  recursive scope expansion sweeps it in — no per-node schedule. The skill's
  job is enrollment (the node's status makes its mode derivable), not
  scheduling.
- The curriculum stays graph-encoded (strategy condition): review items are
  born-parked nodes; /align never maintains a side list.

Landing caveat: `.claude/skills/**` edits are agent-behavior config —
dispatch auto mode denies the commit; land interactively if hit. If
tactic-align-entrypoint-consolidation has landed, the surface is
`.claude/skills/align/SKILL.md`.

Out of scope: the sitting machinery itself
(tactic-review-sitting-skill-generalization); the coverage sensor
(tactic-review-curriculum-coverage-sensor).
