# Observation carrier from the 2026-08-20 /align-tactics tactic-mode round on tactic-autonomous-body-write-wholesale-replace: the sibling write-site roster undercounts dispatch-fleet-alarm by omitting its edit-path body write, and the serving strategy's clarification 245 carries a measurably false claim about dispatch-graph-census — plus three drift observations the round absorbed into its own plan

## What this node is

An **observation carrier**, not planned work. It holds the five immaterial
Side-B drift observations from the 2026-08-20 `/align-tactics` tactic-mode round
that finalized `tactic-autonomous-body-write-wholesale-replace`.

It carries **no plan and no units, and must not be dispatched.** It is born
parked because a human decides what happens to each observation — see
`office_hours.recommendation`, which gives three dispositions rather than one.

**Why a node rather than a strategy clarification.** The round's drift review
classified all five as *immaterial*: the plans do not depend on any of them, so
`proceed` stayed true and the round ran on uninterrupted. Clarification 245 / V1
(ruled 2026-08-14, extended 2026-08-15, which **OVERTURNED** clarification 118)
forbids an autonomous lane landing such observations as dated `clarifications`
on the serving strategy — `clarifications` is allowlist member two of
`strategyFingerprint`, so the write would soft-freeze every open child of the
strategy for an observation defined as gating nothing, and a model-authored
dated clarification is byte-indistinguishable from an author-ruled one.
Separately, `references/tactic-target.md` forbids a per-node session touching the
serving strategy's frontmatter at all. A born-parked observation node serving the
strategy is the one legal destination — the same shape `/align` mints for a
deferral.

Two of the five (A and B) name errors in **records this round could not
correct**: one lives on a sibling draft, the other on a durable-layer node.
Three (C, D, E) were absorbed into the finalized plan and are recorded here only
for their generalizable form.

---

## Observation A — the Layer-3 write-site roster undercounts `dispatch-fleet-alarm`

**Un-absorbed. The recommended route is (1) MECHANIZE.**

`tactic-finding-search-all-producers` (status raw, a draft) holds the
machine-readable write-site roster clarification 244 records as **owed** — the
durable replacement for the prose census that "decays the day it lands". Its
Layer-3 classification records `dispatch-fleet-alarm:705` as **CREATE only**.

Measured this round: `dispatch-fleet-alarm` calls the same wholesale
`splice_body` (defined at `:411`) on its **EDIT** path too — `:790`, inside the
`open)` re-detection refresh case (`:742-800`), guarded only by a `cmp -s`
body-identity check and a `MIN_REFRESH_INTERVAL` rate limit. The roster omits
that call entirely.

**Why it matters beyond one row.** The roster's whole purpose is that a new
write site cannot be added silently. A site that is *present but classified with
one of its two operations missing* is worse than an absent row: it reads as
audited. This is the same failure mode that made the previous round's
five-writer census wrong — enumerating one surface and not asking what else the
caller does.

**Why this round did not fix it.** That roster is another node's scope, and
`tactic-finding-search-all-producers` is a **draft** (`phase: null`) — nothing
autonomous may rewrite a draft's substance. The finalized tactic's body says
explicitly "Do NOT absorb that roster here."

**Generalizable form.** A per-site write classification should be derived from
call sites, not from a reading of what a script is *for*. `dispatch-fleet-alarm`
reads as a minting surface, and its mint call is the one a reviewer's eye lands
on; the refresh call is 80 lines further down in a different case arm.

## Observation B — clarification 245 carries a refuted claim about `dispatch-graph-census`

**Un-absorbed. The recommended route is (2) CLARIFY-ONLY, at an /align interview.**

`strategy-graph-native-dispatch` clarification 245 ends with a paragraph headed
"ONE FURTHER MEASURED FINDING", which states:

> dispatch-graph-census overwrites a census tactic's markdown body wholesale on
> every autonomous run and carries no scope_fingerprint restamp.

The second half is true. **The first half is false, and was false when it was
recorded.** Measured on `origin/main` `43e18881`:

`decideCensus` (`packages/intentionsutil/scripts/graph-census-debt.ts:317-338`)
sets

```
shouldBirth = debt.total >= threshold && debt.openCensus.length === 0 && !idExists
```

where `idExists` tests for the deterministic-per-day id
`tactic-graph-census-<YYYY-MM-DD>`. Its own docstring reads "never clobber an
existing node." That guard landed in `c6fe3c8f` on **2026-07-11** — a month
before the clarification was recorded (2026-08-14/15) and before
`tactic-autonomous-body-write-wholesale-replace` was minted (2026-08-15). The
script cannot reach its body write (`dispatch-graph-census:146-150`) on any node
its own local read can see.

**What survives, and is genuinely a defect:** `idExists` and `openCensus` are
computed from `listNodes()` over the **local** checkout, while the shell probes
`origin/main:intentions/$NODE_ID.md` separately (`dispatch-graph-census:98-102`)
and keeps it as `PRIOR_BLOB`. A checkout stale enough to be missing today's
already-landed census node therefore births again and wholesale-overwrites the
landed body. That is a **stale-read race** — much narrower, and differently
shaped, than "every run after the first." It is scoped as Unit 4 of
`tactic-autonomous-body-write-wholesale-replace`.

**Why this round did not fix it.** A strategy clarification is durable-layer
substance. Clarification 245 itself is the ruling that forbids an autonomous lane
editing it, so correcting it autonomously would be a violation performed inside
the correction of a violation.

**Suggested shape of the fix.** The paragraph's wider point — that a body write
exists with no freeze machinery attached to it, and that its legality rests on
census nodes happening to be tactics — **survives the correction intact**. Only
its example is wrong. Amend the example in place, the way V1 was amended with an
`OVERTURNED` prefix pointing at its successor, rather than deleting the
paragraph.

## Observation C — the harm is measured, not hypothetical (absorbed)

**Absorbed into the finalized plan's §Context and its manual verification
checks 1-2. Recorded here for its evidence.**

The draft framed edit-path body loss as a structural hazard — "neither has been
observed destroying authored content." The round found the ingredients are all
present and routinely co-occur.

`git log` over `intentions/tactic-eval-finding-*.md` shows ledger-node bodies
regularly receive **non-machine** writes: `/align-tactics` finalizes write whole
plans into them (many `graph: finalize tactic-eval-finding-<slug> to phase
implement` commits), plus e.g. `graph: record two drift-review clarifications on
tactic-eval-finding-terminal-without-disposition-dominates-clock` and `graph:
correct reconcile-base-revert-blocks-main-graph-writes diagnosis`.

`dispatch-eval-finding`'s recurrence path refuses only when `execution` is
non-null (`:1207-1211`, the in-flight guard). A node finalized to `phase:
implement` carries `execution: null` until a worker claims it — so a recurrence
landing **in that window** wholesale-replaces an authored plan with the
evaluator's `--body-file` text, silently. That window is a concrete end-to-end
failure scenario a verification step can construct, and the plan's manual check 1
does construct it.

**Generalizable form:** "no instance observed" and "no instrument that would have
observed one" are different claims. The second was the true state here.

## Observation D — two of the three sites cannot take an append arm (absorbed)

**Absorbed into the finalized plan's three-mode design. Recorded here for the
reasoning.**

The draft treated "append below a marked region" and "targeted splice into a
delimited block" as freely choosable per site. Two sites cannot take the append
arm:

- `dispatch-graph-census` documents why in place (`:125-133`): `write-node.ts`
  leaves a generated `# <statement>` placeholder body, so appending produces a
  malformed two-H1 body. The create-vs-edit signal a splice needs is already
  computed one screen above as `PRIOR_BLOB` (`:98-100`), used today only to
  select the EXIT-trap rollback — so the discriminator exists and is unused.
- `dispatch-eval-finding`'s header states the ledger deliberately keeps ONE node
  per finding with its body "refreshed … on every recurrence", precisely so an
  entry carries a recurrence *figure* rather than growing per occurrence. An
  append arm on the recurrence path would reintroduce exactly the unbounded
  growth that design prevents.

Both sites want a **machine-owned region replaced in place** with everything
else preserved — which is why the landed plan carries three modes, not two.

**Generalizable form:** "append vs replace" is decided by whether the generated
content is an accumulating log or a latest reading, and that property belongs to
the *content*, not to the writer.

## Observation E — three live couplings on the same seam (absorbed)

**Absorbed into the finalized plan's Unit 1 blocking note, Unit 3 sequencing
note, Verification check 0, and §Future work. Recorded here for the list.**

1. `test-dispatch-fleet-alarm.sh:330` asserts today's behavior verbatim —
   `assert_eq "(4) refreshed body landed on disk" "A different reading."` — so
   bringing fleet-alarm's refresh into scope **rewrites** that assertion. That is
   an assertion tracking a ratified behavior change, **not** a weakened test
   under `.claude/rules/test-integrity.md`, and the landed plan says so
   explicitly so a later reader cannot mistake it for suppression.
2. `tactic-scope-fingerprint-plan-substance` is **phase qa on PR #2974** and
   introduces `packages/intentionsutil/scripts/append-machinery-section.ts` and
   `packages/intentionsutil/src/body-substance.ts`, both absent from
   `origin/main`. That is the shared home for a machinery-owned body section.
   The plan as returned by the round's Workflow **missed this**; the caller
   thread verified it and added the blocking note. Worth noting as a process
   observation in its own right: a grep against `origin/main` cannot see an
   in-flight sibling, so "grep confirms this is net-new" is only ever a claim
   about `main`.
3. `tactic-graph-write-refresh-clobber-class-observation` (born-parked
   2026-08-20) records an **adjacent but distinct** four-site class — the
   fetch-`origin/main` → overwrite → mutate idiom at `transition-node`,
   `park-node`'s `restore_node`, `clear-park`, and `resolve-hold`'s
   `clean_node_file`, the last of which discards its pre-refresh copy on the
   success path. Same symptom (silent body loss), different mechanism
   (rollback-copy discard, not a body splice). **The two should not be merged
   into one fix.**
