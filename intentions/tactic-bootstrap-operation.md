# Execute the bootstrap operation - implement the greenfield graph/reconciliation machinery through the dispatch-resumption gate under the 2026-09-01 frozen-queue authority grant - as the operation carrier with steering ledger, self-liquidating on completion
## Context

The 2026-09-01 /align rounds ratified the greenfield reconciliation doctrine
(review/qa/main-qa/merge gating), declared its bootstrap shims, and issued the
frozen-queue authority grant with a delegated critical-path articulation - all
on `strategy-graph-native-dispatch`. This operation executes that path: it
exists so the bootstrap has one mechanical claim object, one steering ledger,
and one recorded set of completion criteria. Recommended model: opus - the
operation is claimed by the orchestrating main-thread session itself, never
delegated wholesale; only its implementation units are delegated (per the
steering ledger's working rules).

## Operation scope and claim

The operation claims the bootstrap critical path recorded on
`strategy-graph-native-dispatch`'s execution-authority clarification
(2026-09-01, frozen-queue ruling). That P0-P4 prose is a marked projection:
the `blocked_by` topology on the carrier tactics plus the P4 resumption-gate
criterion are the target form, and the edges win on conflict. The carrier
tactics in scope: `tactic-intent-orchestration-layer-schema`,
`tactic-migration-frontier-projection`, `tactic-consolidation-operation`,
`tactic-ladder-reconciliation-observe`, plus the P2 queue-transcription work
(the minted `tactic-mainqa-*` corpus, the frozen tactic corpus's scope
completion through the reconciliation machinery, and the disposition of
in-flight PRs #3183/#3185 as materialized projections).

Band-park un-park rule object: a band-parked node whose scope this operation
covers is un-parked by that coverage (the rule on
`strategy-graph-native-dispatch` references this node).

Claim encoding, incumbent: this node at `phase: implement` plus the executing
session is the claim; it migrates to the claim-record schema when
`tactic-intent-orchestration-layer-schema` lands. Operational note (instrument
fact, not doctrine): the operation is authored and landed from the
`strategy-explicit-intent` worktree - the node-id⇔worktree claim convention is
relaxed here under the frozen-queue grant, since no worker can race a frozen
queue; `pace_exempt: true` records that the operation is not subject to the
pace curve while queues are frozen.

## Steering ledger protocol

Every author steering directive issued mid-bootstrap is appended to this
node's `clarifications` as a dated `Steering ledger [N]` entry in the same
turn it is given - never batched, never paraphrased into session notes. The
ledger plus claim-time materialization is the ratified steering-loss-
minimization mechanism; on compaction, the ledger is the surviving record.

## Completion criteria

1. **P4 dispatch-resumption gate**: the reconciliation-frontier deriver and
   claim machinery are live, and the transcribed queues validate - a queue
   means something only when derived from graph state. No queue consumer
   unfreezes before this gate.
2. **Terminal liquidation sweep**: the shim inventory is drained (or each
   survivor explicitly re-owned by a standing strategy through interview);
   bootstrap-scoped clarifications on standing strategies are marked
   historical or folded by the consolidation operation; the frozen-queue
   authority grant is recorded expired.
3. **Carrier self-liquidation**: this node completes and prunes; its record
   folds to the git archive. Temporary bootstrap considerations do not
   survive bootstrap completion.

## Shim declaration

Per the bootstrap shim principle (`strategy-graph-native-dispatch`,
2026-09-01): this carrier is a claim-time-produced shim. Target element: the
claim record and work record of the reconciliation model (owned by
`tactic-intent-orchestration-layer-schema`'s schema surface). Liquidation
condition: bootstrap completion - the P4 gate plus the terminal liquidation
sweep discharge it, and the carrier prunes.
