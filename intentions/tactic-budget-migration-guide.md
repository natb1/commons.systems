# Budget migration guide from hosted budgeting tools, plus an honest 'how this is different' section — documentation, not positioning

## Retained concepts (from gh #516, migrated 2026-07-06)

Migration guide (core):

- A migration page covers data export and import from at least Mint, YNAB,
  and Monarch; each path tested end-to-end with a real export from that
  platform.
- Honest about what budget cannot import (if anything) and what the user
  should do about it.
- Titles/meta may describe the page plainly for search intent, but search
  telemetry never drives the content or its structure
  (delegation-web-analytics review trigger: analytics signals driving
  publishing or marketing decisions).

"How this is different" (documentation, not positioning):

- A short section states trade-offs honestly — fewer features than the
  incumbents; your data never leaves your device; no subscription; forkable —
  with no comparison matrix against named incumbents.
- Linked from the budget app; tone follows the brand voice (direct, honest
  about limitations, practical).

Dropped by the issue's own 2026-07-04 re-scope (stands): competitive
positioning against YNAB/Actual/Monarch — the offering posture
strategy-open-source-as-gift rejects; strategy-commons-income keeps artifact
positioning low-rank and gift-terms-only.

Sequencing: the issue noted "file for when the blog-post series has shipped";
gated here on the tier-2 entry declaration on strategy-progressive-validation
like every user-facing invitation. Full original text: gh #516.
