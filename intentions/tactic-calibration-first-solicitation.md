# Solicit a first external calibration challenge — present one live priority or delegation assessment to a zero-stake practitioner and invite pushback, then register the outcome

Born-parked human tactic (2026-07-11 /align-tactics round 1). No implement
plan lives here — the work is the author's: one ~30-minute solicitation ask
inside the ungated community-participation channel, then registration of the
outcome per the Calibration events convention in
`strategy-external-calibration`'s body (authored by
`tactic-calibration-event-registry`, which blocks this tactic).

Why human-only: the calibration function exists precisely because the
audited delegation cannot audit itself — a challenge solicited, filtered, or
paraphrased by the audited vendor's models would re-open the hole this
strategy closes. The author asks, the zero-stake practitioner challenges,
the office-hours review disposes.

Channel constraint: participation venues only (IndieWeb, local-first,
self-hosting — `strategy-join-existing-practice`); no public
call-for-challenges surface while tiers 2/3 of
`strategy-progressive-validation` are un-entered (strategy clarification,
2026-07-11).
