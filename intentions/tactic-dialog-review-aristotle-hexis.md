# Dialog session: Socratic review of reading chunk 2 (tactic-reading-chunk-2-aristotle-hexis, NE II.1-7 — habituation, hexis, the mean) — author recall and origination; carries the chunk-5 sitting's retroactive hexis-pass items R9-R11

One office-hours dialog sitting; the session may span sittings (the
~30-minute bound is the author's independent work — here the recall exercise
itself). First instance of the dialog exercise type
(`strategy-graph-review-curriculum`, 2026-07-18 taxonomy). Reinforces reading
chunk 2: `tactic-reading-chunk-2-aristotle-hexis` (NE II.1–7, done
2026-07-13, landed d85791ad). Companions: `tactic-dialog-review-plato-cave`
(chunk 1), `tactic-dialog-review-aristotle-phronesis` (chunk 5 — the
deferred-ledger ratification gate).

## Conduct

- Socratic dialog; recall and origination focus — the author reproduces the
  doctrine unprompted before any account of Claude's appears. No re-reading
  assumed; probes may cite the text.
- Comprehension (recall/origination) precedes ratification — the 2026-07-13
  staging rule, generalized to dialog sittings.
- Every deferred item below is held under the author's 2026-07-18 glare
  ruling ("all of these outcomes sound correct but there is still too much
  platonic glare to ratify outright"); pre-pivot in-sitting statuses are
  preserved as signal, not landed state. Each item resolves: **ratify** (land
  on its target durable node in the sitting's own graph-commit), **amend**
  (author wording wins), or **re-defer** (name the next review home).
- Recursive frontier duty: the sitting itself identifies follow-up
  references, vocabulary, and quotes for future reinforcement (the
  two-feeder frontier rule on `strategy-graph-review-curriculum`).

## Agenda

### 1. Recall and origination — chunk-2 vocabulary and quotes

Vocabulary (recall meaning and locus unprompted): *hexis* (II.5 genus);
*energeia*; *ethos/ēthos* (the 1103a17 pun); *pathos/dynamis/hexis*;
*mesotēs* with *hyperbolē/elleipsis/meson*; *ho phronimos* (II.6's IOU,
since paid by chunk 5).

Quotes (recall substance and why each is load-bearing): 1103a33–b2 (builders
by building, just by doing just acts); 1105b12–18 (patients who hear the
doctor); 1104b3–8 (pleasure supervening on the act as the sign — R11's
ground); 1106b36–1107a2 (the full definition, as the phronimos would define
it).

Origination probes: reproduce the chunk-2 landed outcomes — habituation
ratified (the teacher turns the practice; the II.4 circle entered by chance
or guidance); the mean requalified intra-virtue; the cadence-as-habituation
amendment (synchronization drift, not decay-by-inactivity).

If confirmed, retention entries land durably on `tradition-aristotle` (never
on this prunable node) per the 2026-07-15 retention requirement.

### 2. Retroactive integration items R9–R11 (minted at the chunk-5 sitting, hexis pass)

- **R9 — the habituation layer.** Rules/skills/hooks are the habituation
  layer for a sessionless delegatee — they transmit no knowledge
  constituting the virtue; they turn the practice. "The environment, not
  instruction, is where a sessionless delegatee's hexis lives." Pre-pivot:
  ratified. Target: `kind-delegation`.
- **R10 — Milo** (1106a36–b4) grounds R3 (sensor-drift surfacing, cave
  dialog): the right interval is relative to the record, fixed by judgment,
  never a one-size-fits-all timeout. Pre-pivot: ratified. Target: rides with
  R3.
- **R11 — pleasure-as-sign, the derived-sign mechanism** (1104b3–8).
  Continent and virtuous agents produce identical edit histories, so
  revealed preference cannot discriminate; the felt sign is the only
  discriminator. No checkbox — two behaviorally-computable signs:
  **voluntary overshoot** and **unforced latency**; judgment stays
  human/retrospective at review; tooling is report-only. Pre-pivot: ratified
  (locked, "confirmed with new mechanism"). Target:
  `strategy-graph-review-curriculum`. R15a (the habituation mechanism
  generalizes; the continence trap — habit apps are continence machines,
  streak-as-scaffolding-that-relaxes; log acts never feelings; environment
  over willpower plus external review) rides with it — reviewed at the
  phronesis dialog.
