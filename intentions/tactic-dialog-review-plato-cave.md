# Dialog session: Socratic review of reading chunk 1 (Plato, Republic VII — the cave; node tactic-reading-chunk-1-plato-cave, pruned after done, outcomes durable on tradition-plato) — author recall and origination; carries the chunk-5 sitting's retroactive cave-pass items R1-R5

One office-hours dialog sitting; the session may span sittings (the
~30-minute bound is the author's independent work — here the recall exercise
itself). First instance of the dialog exercise type
(`strategy-graph-review-curriculum`, 2026-07-18 taxonomy). Reinforces reading
chunk 1: `tactic-reading-chunk-1-plato-cave` (Republic VII 514a–521b, done
2026-07-07, landed 98d20919; the chunk node was pruned by a later census
sweep — enrollment anchors on `tradition-plato`, the durable home of its
outcomes). Companions: `tactic-dialog-review-aristotle-hexis` (chunk 2),
`tactic-dialog-review-aristotle-phronesis` (chunk 5 — the deferred-ledger
ratification gate).

## Conduct

- Socratic dialog; recall and origination focus — the author reproduces the
  doctrine unprompted before any account of Claude's appears. No re-reading
  assumed; probes may cite the text.
- Comprehension (recall/origination) precedes ratification — the 2026-07-13
  staging rule, generalized to dialog sittings.
- Every deferred item below is held under the author's 2026-07-18 glare
  ruling ("all of these outcomes sound correct but there is still too much
  platonic glare to ratify outright"); pre-pivot in-sitting statuses are
  preserved as signal, not landed state. Each item resolves: **ratify** (land
  on its target durable node in the sitting's own graph-commit), **amend**
  (author wording wins), or **re-defer** (name the next review home).
- Recursive frontier duty: the sitting itself identifies follow-up
  references, vocabulary, and quotes for future reinforcement (the
  two-feeder frontier rule on `strategy-graph-review-curriculum`).

## Agenda

### 1. Recall and origination — chunk-1 vocabulary and quotes

Vocabulary (recall meaning and locus unprompted): *periagōgē* (518d);
*paideia* (518b–c); *organon* (518c); *idea tou agathou* (517b–c).

Quotes (recall substance and why each is load-bearing): 518b–d (not putting
sight into blind eyes — turning the whole soul); 518e–519a (the faculty
never loses its power; by its turning it becomes useful or harmful); 517a
(they would kill the liberator).

Origination probes: reproduce the misdirects-not-corrupts amendment and what
it changed in capture doctrine; the agency-of-turning account; the Form of
the Good as external anchor; descent-as-reentry.

If confirmed, retention entries land durably on `tradition-plato` (never on
this prunable node) per the 2026-07-15 retention requirement.

### 2. Retroactive integration items R1–R5 (minted at the chunk-5 sitting, cave pass)

- **R1 — glare period** (Rep. 515e–516a). Doctrine is least trustworthy
  right after amendment. Author framing (verbatim, wording wins): "that is
  why the graph is expected to have a strategy for periodic review at
  broader context and from different perspectives… with repeated exposure
  the author may ratify truths that could not be seen in a prior pass."
  Pre-pivot: ratified (author's wording). Target:
  `strategy-graph-review-curriculum` (grounding clarification).
- **R2 — return to the cave** (519c–520d). The ascended are compelled back
  down; the return-obligation grounds the gift/practice strategies; one-line
  adopted entry. Open sub-deferral: the interplay with the Kantian
  categorical imperative — flagged on
  `tactic-reading-chunk-31-kant-autonomy-heteronomy`. Pre-pivot: ratified,
  sub-deferral open. Target: `tradition-plato` (adopted entry).
- **R3 — sensor-drift surfacing.** The coverage sensor surfaces
  `last_exercised`/`last_assessed` staleness; author (wording wins): "the
  timestamp is useful metadata, but there is no rule for how long is too
  long… remediation cannot be automated using some one size fits all
  timeout." Surface-only, no threshold. Pre-pivot: ratified as amended.
  Target: a tooling_goals entry / born-parked draft tactic. R10 (Milo: the
  right interval is relative to the record, fixed by judgment) rides with it
  — reviewed at the hexis dialog.
- **R4 — III.4 both/and** (1113a23–25) makes capture diagnosable; adopted
  entry plus capture-doctrine reframe: "capture corrupts appearance, not
  wishing: the not-good appears good, and the choosing apparatus keeps
  working on the false appearance." Pre-pivot: ratified (reframed). Target:
  `tradition-aristotle` (adopted) + `virtue-philosophical-mobility` rationale
  reframe. Cave-pass item (it completes the misdirects account); the III.4
  text is chunk 5's — cross-referenced on the phronesis dialog agenda.
- **R5 — the spoudaios has no tribunal.** "Aristotle gives the spoudaios no
  tribunal above him — the measure is self-certifying. The graph refuses
  exactly this (mobility's calibration is external by doctrine)." Resolution
  drafted: NOT a new entry — amend the existing fallibilism divergence on
  `tradition-aristotle` with the III.4 locus and the sharp form; corroborated
  by 1143b11–14 (others get reason to trust the phronimos; the phronimos
  never gets reason to doubt himself). Pre-pivot: drafted amendment, queued,
  never re-confirmed. Target: `tradition-aristotle` (amend the fallibilism
  divergence). Cross-referenced on the phronesis dialog agenda.
