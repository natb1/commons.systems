## What this node is

A **born-parked observation carrier**. It holds the four immaterial Side-B drift
observations returned by the 2026-08-20 `/align-tactics` tactic-mode round on
`tactic-dispatch-conflict-substance-allowlist`. It carries **no plan, no phase,
no units and no verification** — there is nothing here for an implement worker
to build, and it must not be dispatched.

**Why it exists rather than the observations being written to the strategy.**
`strategy-graph-native-dispatch` clarification 245 (violation **V1**, ruled
2026-08-14 and extended 2026-08-15) redirects a round's immaterial drift away
from a direct `clarifications` write on the serving strategy and into exactly
one born-parked observation node serving it — so `proceed` stays true and the
round runs on uninterrupted, while no autonomous lane edits durable-layer
substance. That ruling **overturned** clarification 118, which had permitted a
per-node session to append clarifications to the serving strategy; 118 now
carries an `OVERTURNED 2026-08-15` prefix pointing at 245. Independently,
`references/tactic-target.md` forbids a per-node tactic-target session from
touching the serving strategy's frontmatter at all. A human promotes whatever is
worth promoting into `clarifications` at office hours, through the attended
surface.

**None of the four gated the round.** `drift.proceed` was `true`,
`side_a_failed_conditions` was empty, `parks` was empty, `deviation` was `false`.
Observations 2 and 3 were absorbed into the finalized plan body on
`tactic-dispatch-conflict-substance-allowlist` and are recorded here only for
their generalizable form. Observations 1 and 4 have no other home.

## Observation 1 — the maintenance-burden band reads out of band, and its own numerator is the reason

**This is the one that needs the author.**

The strategy's maintenance-burden band condition declares that the open
machinery-defect population — open (phase set, not done) plus born-parked
tactics serving this strategy — stays at or below **35%** of all tactics serving
it, and is non-increasing across consecutive samples. It was armed 2026-08-05 at
59/197 = 30.0%, and the strategy's `reading` records 58/236 = 24.6% on
2026-08-10.

Measured independently on this round's worktree (cut fresh from `origin/main`),
using the repo's own sensor function — `strategyBacklogBand`,
`packages/intentionsutil/src/census.ts:26-40`, the same function
`read-sensors.ts` uses to compose the recorded reading:

```
backlog 115  total 311  pct 37.0%
  open (phase set, not done)  79
  born-parked                 36
```

**37.0% is above the declared 35% ceiling, and 24.6% → 37.0% is increasing.** On
its face that is the condition failing, which its own text says parks the
strategy for an author decision.

The drift review did **not** declare it failed, and the reason is that the
numerator is confounded by nodes of exactly this kind. Of the 36 born-parked
nodes, **13 are observation/drift carriers** minted by the V1 redirect:

```
tactic-attributes-phase-squatter-drift-observations
tactic-fingerprint-sha-provenance-drift-observations
tactic-fleet-watch-cadence-drift-observations
tactic-graph-auto-merge-behind-arm-drift-observations
tactic-graph-auto-merge-hold-observability-drift-observations
tactic-graph-plumbing-flip-drift-observations
tactic-graph-write-refresh-clobber-class-observation
tactic-harness-precondition-round-observations
tactic-observation-draft-anchor-decay
tactic-observation-ladder-terminus-baseline-drift
tactic-optin-gated-widening-drift-observations
tactic-scope-stamp-coverage-out-of-graph-coupling-observation
tactic-verify-landed-unknown-arm-drift-observations
```

Excluding them gives **102/311 = 32.8% — in band.**

Three things make this a genuine measurement problem rather than a convenient
excuse:

1. **The comparison is not apples-to-apples.** The V1 redirect landed
   **2026-08-15** — *after* the band was armed (2026-08-05) and *after* the last
   recorded reading (2026-08-10). Every carrier in the numerator today is a node
   class the band's author never contemplated when declaring 35%.
2. **The sensor cannot tell a carrier from a parked defect.** `classifyTactic`
   (`packages/intentionsutil/src/census.ts:13-18`) scores every `phase`-null node
   with `office_hours` set as `born-parked` backlog. A node the convention itself
   describes as *deliberately unplanned record-keeping* is scored as an open
   machinery defect. There is no mark to key on.
3. **The convention ratchets the ratio upward by construction.** Every round that
   returns immaterial drift mints one more carrier, adding +1 to both numerator
   and denominator — which raises the ratio whenever the ratio is below 1. That
   is monotone pressure on the band that is independent of whether the real
   maintenance burden is growing at all. **This node is itself an instance:**
   minting it moved the measurement.

Recorded, not decided: the drift review is not the surface that may rule a
recorded condition failed or not-failed — conditions are human-decided. What it
can honestly say is that the raw number is out of band, that the overshoot is
fully accounted for by a node class postdating the declaration, and that the
adjusted number is in band. The disposition is the author's; see this node's
`office_hours.recommendation` for the three options.

## Observation 2 — three divergent "durable layer" kind sets, and the exported one is the wrong one

Absorbed into `tactic-dispatch-conflict-substance-allowlist`'s plan (Unit 1);
recorded here for the generalizable form.

Three non-identical memberships travel under two names:

| Site | Membership | Exported? |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `packages/intentionsutil/src/grounding.ts:26-30` (`DURABLE_KINDS`) | virtue, strategy, kind, delegation | **yes** |
| `packages/intentionsutil/src/coverage.ts:32-38` (`DURABLE_KINDS`) | virtue, strategy, kind, tradition, delegation | no |
| `.claude/skills/dispatch-conflict/SKILL.md:576-582` (doctrine prose) | virtue, strategy, tradition, delegation | n/a |

Clarification 244's ratified set for the autonomous-substance invariant is
**virtue, strategy, delegation, kind, tradition** — which matches `coverage.ts`
exactly, and matches neither of the other two.

The trap is that `grounding.ts`'s is the **only exported** one, so it is the one
an implementer reaches for by default — and it omits `tradition` *on purpose*
("tradition-\* records ARE the grounding"), answering a different question
entirely. Importing it into a substance gate would leave tradition records
unguarded, silently, while looking like correct reuse. The SKILL.md prose omits
`kind`, a third variant.

The generalizable form: **two constants with the same name and different
membership, one exported and one not, is worse than either alone** — the export
decides which one wins by accident of accessibility rather than by fit. Unifying
the three behind one canonical exported constant is worth doing beyond the one
gate this round scoped it to.

## Observation 4 — `graph-commit` documents an exit code it does not have

Deserves its own tactic; small, self-contained, two-line fix.

`packages/intentionsutil/scripts/graph-commit` states in its header (`:169`)
that "`--base` staleness keeps exit 3", and `emit_verdict_and_exit`'s own comment
(`:2429`) repeats that staleness does not route through it. **There is no `exit
3` statement anywhere in the script** — a repository search finds only those two
comments plus an unrelated convention note at `:1053`. Every non-landed verdict,
stale `--base` included, exits **1** via `emit_verdict_and_exit` (`:2432-2439`).

Consequence: a caller that trusts the header and case-dispatches on exit 3 will
silently never match, and will fold a stale-base refusal into whatever its
generic exit-1 branch does.

This is also the concrete evidence for why
`tactic-dispatch-conflict-substance-allowlist` is right to model its
refuse-before-mutation contract on `park-node`'s `--base` rather than
`graph-commit`'s: `park-node:355-362` has a real dedicated `exit 3` that runs
before any mutation and says "Nothing was written.", whereas `graph-commit`'s
`check_base_freshness()` comments that it will "Attempt a structural three-way
merge instead of refusing." The contrast the tactic's rationale draws is not
merely stylistic — one of the two documented contracts does not exist.

Fixing the comment (or adding the exit) is separate scope from that tactic and
from this carrier.
