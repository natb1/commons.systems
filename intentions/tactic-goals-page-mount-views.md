# Goals-page mount exploration — render mount boundaries, hand-assessed and derived degree, and the motivating-dependencies query view

Interview context retained verbatim for /align-tactics (2026-07-07 round that
recorded strategy-graph-mounts):

- **Surface.** The office-hours goals page owned by strategy-attention-surface
  (its rationale already promises "where delegation and capture concentrate");
  this tactic supplies the mount-aware version of those views, coordinating
  with the tactic-attention-surface-* family rather than adding a new app.
- **Views required by the strategy's success_signal:**
  - mount boundaries rendered as boundaries — a mounted subtree visibly
    distinct from native structure, attributed to its anchoring
    delegation/tradition/person record;
  - degree display: the record's hand-assessed level next to the derived
    boundary-flow degree, disagreement highlighted as a review signal;
  - the motivating-dependencies query: from any strategy, enumerate every
    mounted virtue/strategy/duty that motivates it. Canonical acceptance
    case: strategy-financial-sustainability surfaces the grafted commercial
    growth virtue;
  - deferral to traditions explorable the same way (adopted/diverged/
    chosen_over as structure once tactic-mount-schema migrates them);
  - recursion: nested mounts (vendor → governing body) explorable without
    flattening the boundaries.
- **Read path.** Local-first per strategy-attention-surface: browser graph
  read layer over the local clone; derived degree comes from tooling
  (tactic-mount-schema's sensor), not computed ad hoc in the browser if the
  computation needs history.
