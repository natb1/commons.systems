## Observation 1 — a held-for-office-hours watcher predicate is NOT quiet under a standing pause

A predicate that reports merge candidates held out of the auto-merge queue by a park belongs
to `dispatch-fleet-watch`'s **not-quiet-under-pause** class, alongside daemon-liveness,
auto-merge-suppression and unclaimed-hold — not the quiet class (tick-staleness, sustained
`BUSY=0`).

This is a *derivation* from the 2026-07-31 pause-quietness ruling, not a new author decision.
That ruling silences only checks whose condition is **caused** by the pause and would
therefore fire continuously through a supported standing operating mode (during a pause,
`dispatch-tick` exits before `dispatch-select-tick` runs, so tick-staleness and `BUSY=0` are
guaranteed to trip). A pause creates no parks, so a held-for-office-hours condition does not
arise from the pause.

What makes the mapping worth recording is that it now runs the other way too:
tactic-pause-disables-merge-lane (PR #3068) puts the node-lane merge lane on
`dispatch-tick`'s **paused** branch (`dispatch-tick:649-655` invokes `graph-auto-merge`
there), so the park's merge veto is **live during a standing pause**. A mass false-park
would drain the queue in exactly the mode where manual dispatch most needs the surface. The
2026-07-31 ruling settles the principle but predates that merge-lane change, so the specific
mapping was unrecorded.

The same header's fail direction applies unchanged: an unreadable pause state reports
UNKNOWN and **still emits**. `dispatch-fleet-watch:107-121` already documents the split.

**Generalizable form:** the pause-quietness test is "is this condition *caused* by the
pause?", not "does this condition involve dispatch?". Every future fleet-watch predicate has
to answer that question, and the answer is not inferable from the predicate's subject
matter.

## Observation 2 — "escalate" on a park is bounded to reporting

The word "escalate" in the target tactic's statement means **reporting** — a find-or-create
fleet-alarm graph node — and may never expire, demote, or auto-clear the park so the PR
merges.

Three recorded constraints converge on this, and none is being reopened:

1. The 2026-08-05 one-gate ruling (clarification 198) records as a **standing invariant of
   the merge path** that a withheld node is not merge-eligible, whatever the form of the
   withholding.
2. The 2026-07-31 ALARM SURFACE ruling requires an out-of-band instrument's finding — **and
   its UNKNOWN reading** — to land as a graph node rather than journald alone.
3. That ruling's NEVER FLEET-HALT clause forbids the instrument tripping the
   correlated-dead-claims breaker or otherwise halting selection.

The live gate's own comment states the same posture: "Decline VISIBLY and never demote"
(`.claude/skills/dispatch-propagate/scripts/graph-auto-merge:322-330`). Clarification 98
independently rules that mechanical retry holds written as parks are a **producer**-side
defect with its own disposition (tactic-mechanical-park-producers, `phase: done`), not
something a consumer may unwind.

So the target tactic's scope is strictly additive: a new `dispatch-fleet-watch` predicate
plus one entry appended to `dispatch-fleet-alarm`'s closed `KINDS` enum
(`dispatch-fleet-alarm:181`), with **no change to `graph-auto-merge`'s gate behavior**.
Following the 2026-08-13 casualty-list doctrine, the alarm body names the held node ids, not
merely a count.

**Generalizable form:** an observability tactic filed against a gate must state, in its own
scope, that it does not touch the gate. Without that the word "escalate" reads as licence to
unblock.

## Observation 3 — "across many consecutive ticks" names a symptom, not a mandated counter

The choice between a tick-count and a wall-clock age source, and between graph-derived state
and a sidecar counter file, stays **plan-level**.

This extends the identical 2026-07-31 ruling made for the pending-CI liveness bound
(clarification 159), which expressly left that choice to the plan while recording the pause
interaction so a later round would not rediscover it.

Recorded here because the cheap option is already available and should be considered first:
`packages/intentionsutil/scripts/park-node:377` stamps
`office_hours = { reason, since, recommendation }` on **every** park, so every held candidate
already carries a first-class `since` clock, and
`packages/intentionsutil/src/hold-alerts.ts:52-63` documents the resulting **stateless**
property — two passes over the same graph agree without remembering anything. A new
cross-pass `STATE_FILE` stamp would additionally need per-node keying, which
`dispatch-fleet-watch`'s existing scalar since-stamps (`busy_zero_since`,
`suppression_since`) do not provide as-is.

Measured caveat the finalize round added, load-bearing for any threshold built on it:
`office_hours.since` is **day granularity**. `schema.ts:702-707` types `OfficeHours` as
`{reason, since, recommendation, session_type}` and `schema.ts:930` validates `since`
through `requireDateString` (`^\d{4}-\d{2}-\d{2}$`, `schema.ts:741-747`). No duration
threshold below ~1 day is expressible from that clock, and a park set today reads as age 0
for the rest of the day.

**Generalizable form:** a statement authored by a review finder may name a mechanism
("consecutive ticks") that does not exist. The finalize round's job is to discharge the
*symptom* and say so in the body, not to build the named mechanism.

## Observation 4 — the directly-parked merge candidate is not the hold-node shape

Two boundary facts, so a plan does not misroute through adjacent machinery.

**First, this is not the hold-node shape.** `packages/intentionsutil/src/holds.ts:36-60`
models a hold as a **separate born-parked node** carrying `attributes.hold_kind` /
`hold_for` and blocking a different source via `blocked_by`, with `HOLD_KINDS` still exactly
`[provision-conflict, fix-attempt-cap, worktree-residue]`. `graph-auto-merge`'s veto is the
review node's **own** `office_hours` field, set directly on the merge candidate — no hold
node, no `hold_kind`, no edge. So it must not become a `HOLD_KINDS` entry, and it must not
claim `RESERVED_KIND_SLUGS`' unimplemented `no-progress` slug, which the 2026-07-31
reservation ruling (clarification 158) assigns to the terminal-trichotomy fuse breaker
(tactic-router-failure-fuses, still `status: raw` / `phase: null`).

**Second, imitate the shipped triple rather than extend it.**
tactic-unclaimed-hold-alerting's triple (`src/hold-alerts.ts` +
`scripts/list-unclaimed-hold-alerts.ts` + `dispatch-fleet-watch` predicate 5) is the right
*shape*, but its enumerator is scoped to hold nodes, and extending it would inherit its two
open defects — tactic-hold-alerts-unbounded-scan-cadence and
tactic-hold-alerts-uncapped-alert-rows, both `status: raw` / `phase: null`. Any new
predicate must bound its own scan cadence to its threshold granularity and cap its own
alert-row/body size rather than repeat them.

**Generalizable form:** "reuse the existing alerting machinery" is ambiguous between
*imitate the pattern* and *extend the instance*. When the instance carries open defects,
extending it silently adopts them; the reuse discipline should name which of the two it
means.
