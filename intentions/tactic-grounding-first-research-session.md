# First grounding-research session: walk the initial ranked gap report at office-hours — circumstantial marks, none-found marks, candidate-chunk queues

One office-hours sitting, at most 30 author-minutes. Not claude-executable:
`attributes.grounding` / `attributes.traditions` marks and `/deep-research`
sourcing are author-side only (`strategy-complete-grounding` clarification
4) — this is the author-side half of the round whose worker-side halves are
`tactic-grounding-gap-analysis` (the sensor) and
`tactic-grounding-research-skill` (the session's skill); the `blocked_by`
edges hold this until both land.

At the sitting:

- Run `/grounding-research`; it refreshes the ranked gap report and walks
  the unmarked durable-layer nodes top-down.
- Expect a mix of outcomes: `circumstantial` marks on author-particular
  nodes, `none-found` marks where sourcing comes back empty, and
  `/deep-research`-sourced candidate curriculum chunks (chunks-10–17
  convention) for genuinely tradition-shaped gaps.
- Stop at the sitting bound; the remaining ranking stays for later sessions —
  re-running the skill picks up where the report says the gap still is.
