# Observation carrier — 2026-08-20 harness-precondition finalize round

## What this node is

A **born-parked observation carrier**. It holds five observations produced by
the 2026-08-20 per-node `/align-tactics` finalize of
`tactic-test-park-node-deps-precondition-guard`, none of which an autonomous

It carries **no plan** and **must not be dispatched** to an implement lane.
Its whole purpose is to sit in the office-hours queue until a human
dispositions each item. `phase` is deliberately `null`.

**Why it exists rather than a clarifications write.**
`strategy-graph-native-dispatch` clarification 118 once permitted a per-node
session to append clarifications to the serving strategy. It is prefixed
`OVERTURNED 2026-08-15`. Clarification 245 — violation V1 of the
autonomous-substance invariant, ruled 2026-08-14 and extended 2026-08-15 —
binds instead: the immaterial-drift path mints one born-parked observation node
serving the strategy, and the round proceeds uninterrupted. Three reasons,
recorded so a future round does not re-litigate them:

1. `clarifications` is an allowlist member of `strategyFingerprint`, so an
   autonomous append soft-freezes **every** open child of the strategy — for
   observations defined as gating nothing.
2. It is a second requirement-entry surface that
   `strategy-discovered-requirements` reserves to the `/align` interview.
3. A model-authored dated clarification is byte-indistinguishable from an
   author-ruled one, so provenance in the field that carries doctrine collapses
   irreversibly.

A tactic-target session additionally never touches the serving strategy's
frontmatter at all (`.claude/skills/align-tactics/references/tactic-target.md`).

**Why not a park on the target instead.** The autonomy contract's three park
conditions — requirement ambiguity, major scope deviation, unverifiable blocker
— none fit. The finalize round completed: `drift.proceed` was true, no Side A
condition failed, and the plan landed. Parking the target would have been false.

## Item 3 — CI wiring is uneven across the ten symlink sites

The finalize round measured ten harnesses carrying the identical unguarded
`ln -s "$REAL_REPO_ROOT/node_modules"`. Only **three** are invoked by
`.github/workflows/unit-tests.yml`:

- CI-wired: `test-park-node.sh`, `test-arm-wait.sh`, `test-release-wait.sh`
- **Not invoked by any workflow**: `test-hold-node.sh`, and all six
  `.claude/skills/dispatch-propagate/scripts/` harnesses
  (`test-graph-write-rollback.sh`, `test-lib-wait-recheck.sh`,
  `test-resolve-hold.sh`, `test-lib-stale-hold-recheck.sh`,
  `test-dispatch-graph-main-red-sync.sh`,
  `test-dispatch-invalid-state-followup.sh`)

Consequence for the round that surfaced it: the guard added at seven of the ten
sites gets no functional CI exercise. Only the new ratchet
(`tactic-test-park-node-deps-precondition-guard` Unit 3) gives their guard
*presence* any CI coverage at all.

This is adjacent to the known "skill shell tests are not autodiscovered in CI"
gap and was **deliberately not fixed** by that tactic — wiring seven harnesses
into CI as functional suites is a different, larger change with its own
runtime and flake budget.

**The real question is not mechanical**: are those seven meant to be CI-wired?
A harness nobody runs is either a gap to close or dead weight to delete, and
which one it is has not been decided. Do not mechanize this until it has been.

## Item 4 — `lint-prose-rules.sh` structurally cannot express whole-file invariants

`.claude/skills/dispatch-propagate/scripts/lint-prose-rules.sh` is the repo's
existing shell-rule enforcement point, run via `run-lint.sh` in CI. It is
structurally a **net-new-added-lines diff scanner**: it diffs
`origin/main...HEAD --unified=0` and inspects only added lines.

That shape cannot express an invariant of the form *"if this file contains X,
it must also contain Y, earlier in the file"* — which is exactly the shape the
harness-guard uniformity rule needs. It is why
`tactic-test-park-node-deps-precondition-guard` adds a ratchet harness (Unit 3)
rather than a lint rule.

Recorded because the constraint is **general**, not specific to that tactic:
every future whole-file or ordering invariant hits the same wall and will
reach for the same workaround. Worth knowing before someone designs a third
one, and worth deciding whether the repo wants a second, file-scoped linter
alongside the diff-scoped one.

## Item 5 — The align-tactics Workflow's drift and plan phases disagreed on scope

Internal-consistency observation about the machinery itself, not about the
target.

In this round the Workflow's **drift** phase recorded (as immaterial premise 2)
that the target's statement "scopes the fix to `test-park-node.sh` only, so its
plan names the siblings as explicitly out of scope rather than widening."

The Workflow's **plan** phase then authored a three-unit plan whose Unit 2 is
*"Add the same guard to the other nine measured sites"* and whose Unit 3 adds a
CI-wired ratchet across all ten — the opposite disposition.

**How the caller thread resolved it, and why:** in favour of widening. The
target node's own draft `## Scope` section explicitly invited the sibling
question — *"Consider whether the sibling harnesses that use the same
`make_clone` shape share the gap; if so, the guard belongs somewhere they can
all reach rather than copy-pasted."* That is recorded scope, so answering it is
not a deviation from the node's substance; the drift phase read the statement
without weighing that bullet. The statement was the narrower of the two and was
amended in this same round to match the plan, per the whole-node reconciliation
bar (condition 8 / clarification 32). The plan's rejection of the draft's
preferred *mechanism* (a shared bash home) is separately argued in the target's
body and respects the draft's intent — uniformity without copy-paste drift —
via a ratchet instead.

Nothing was lost. But the two phases reaching opposite dispositions from the
same inputs, with no field in the returned result flagging the disagreement, is
the kind of thing that is only caught because a human-facing thread happened to
read both. If it recurs it is worth mechanizing a check;
`tactic-align-tactics-immaterial-drift-redirect` is the nearest existing
carrier for align-tactics Workflow corrections.
