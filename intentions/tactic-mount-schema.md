# Mount data structure — anchors on delegation/tradition records, mounted-node residence, the distinct graft relation, derived degree, recursion, by-reference readiness, validate rules

Interview context retained verbatim for /align-tactics (2026-07-07 round that
recorded strategy-graph-mounts). Decisions already fixed by the strategy's
clarifications — the schema must realize them, not relitigate them:

- **Records anchor mounts.** Each existing `delegation-*` / `tradition-*`
  record becomes or carries the mount anchor for its counterparty; the prose
  `divergence.imported` and `adopted/diverged` lists migrate into mounted
  subgraph structure over time. One system, no parallel ledger. New
  counterparty classes (persons, families, client/employer, governing
  institutions) get records of the same family.
- **Mounted nodes.** A mounted graph's virtues, strategies, and duties become
  nodes (the canonical example: commercial institutions' virtue of growth,
  currently only a prose string). Residence/naming must distinguish mounted
  nodes from native ones and attribute each to its mount anchor.
- **Distinct graft relation.** `serves` stays native-only (validateGraph
  rule 8 intact). A separate relation carries motivation across the mount
  boundary — e.g. strategy-financial-sustainability motivated by the mounted
  growth virtue — so native-vs-grafted is queryable by relation type.
- **Degree, twice.** Hand-assessed verdicts stay on the record
  (divergence.level, classification). Derived degree is computed from
  motivation flow across the boundary (count/weight of graft edges into
  native strategies). Disagreement between the two is a first-class review
  signal.
- **Recursion.** A mounted graph may itself carry mounts (family mounts its
  individuals; a vendor mounts a governing body or another vendor). By-model
  recursion is nested modeling to audit depth.
- **By-reference readiness.** Nothing in the structure may assume same-repo
  residence: the private-root inversion (this graph mounted on the author's
  private graph), household members' graphs, and align-init fork practitioners
  must all be representable as the same shape with a different store behind
  the mount.
- **Validate rules.** validate-graph gains rules over mount structure (graft
  edges resolve, mounted nodes attribute to an anchor, serves stays native).

Duties: for social mounts (family, client/employer per
delegation-client-income), the mounted graph carries duties as well as
virtues/strategies — the schema needs a home for obligation-shaped content,
not just adopted-virtue content.
