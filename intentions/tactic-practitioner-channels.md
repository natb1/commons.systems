# Identify the practitioner profile and the non-engagement channels to reach them, and fold per-post syndication into a reusable runbook

## Retained concepts (from gh #538 and the 2026-07-06 interview)

- The author's open question, itself part of the tier-3 entry test: who is
  the ideal practitioner (profile) and where are they reachable (channels)
  without engagement platforms?
- Constraints carried from #538's re-scope: no platform-first submission
  protocols, no engagement-window or comment-campaign mechanics
  (delegation-social-publishing's review trigger;
  strategy-recover-publishing's threshold — no piece is platform-first); no
  metrics template driving publishing decisions (delegation-web-analytics).
- Venue participation limited to the communities tactic-join-indieweb names
  (IndieWeb, local-first, self-hosting) as sustained participation, not
  submission campaigns. Participation itself is ungated under
  strategy-join-existing-practice; it is the practitioner-directed
  campaigning this tactic covers that waits on tier 3.
- Runbook concept: consolidate per-post syndication into one reusable
  runbook implementing tactic-indieweb-audience (POSSE with canonical links
  back, feed discoverability, webmentions), linked from each post rather
  than repeated inline.
