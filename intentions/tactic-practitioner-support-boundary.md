# Write the practitioner support boundary — what /align-init and the entry point tell forkers is supported, best-effort, and not supported

## Retained concepts (from gh #2452, migrated 2026-07-06)

- A short support-boundary statement placed where forkers will see it: the
  practitioner entry point and/or the `/align-init` output.
- States what is supported, what is explicitly best-effort, and what is not
  supported at all; honest about no implied SLA and no commitment beyond
  what is real.
- Designated first step of tier-3 entry (strategy-progressive-validation's
  tier-entry test): written before any invitation ships — before plugin
  publication, citation-list submissions, or channel campaigns.
