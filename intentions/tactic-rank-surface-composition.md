# Design the per-surface rank composition — dispatch readiness/cost, alarm blast radius, review expected-impact, context relevance — kept outside the importance backbone

## Retained interview context (2026-08-31 /align doctrine-alignment round)

Four consumers of the one importance backbone, each with its own composition
factor: dispatch (readiness, cost), alarms (blast radius), author review
(expected review impact = importance x expected change), context injection
(task relevance). The review-queue composition coordinates with
tactic-review-debt-reprioritization; the injection composition with
tactic-context-materialization. Virtues stay incommensurable roots —
no design may introduce a virtue-to-virtue comparison.
