# Reading chunk 17 (candidate): the Great Learning and Analects 1-2 — the household as the primary site of cultivation, against the all-Western roster

One office-hours sitting, at most 30 author-minutes. Candidate chunk of
`strategy-complete-grounding`'s first batch: no tradition record exists yet —
this sitting decides whether one should.

## Text

*The Great Learning* (Daxue) — the eight steps (the complete short text).
Confucius, *Analects*, books 1–2.

## Questions to establish relevance

- The household-consent doctrine on `virtue-respect-for-persons` currently
  reads through Kant alone (servility, with-never-for). Confucian relational
  ethics treats the household as the *primary* site of cultivation — the
  Great Learning orders self-cultivation before regulating the family before
  anything wider. Does that support, sharpen, or contradict the graph's
  household-as-equal-persons doctrine?
- Li (ritual propriety) as habituation against Aristotelian hexis: the same
  habituation doctrine in another vocabulary, or a rival account in which
  form precedes disposition?
- The tradition roster is entirely Western — `kind-tradition`'s audit asks
  whether the graph can state its commitments in more than one tradition's
  terms. Does examining a non-Western tradition change the audit posture,
  whatever this candidate's outcome?
