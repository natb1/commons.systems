## Text

Plato, *Theaetetus* 148e–151d — Socratic midwifery: Socrates barren of
wisdom, delivering others' thoughts, testing whether the offspring is
wind-egg or genuine; the god compels him to midwife, not to beget.

Plato, *Meno* 79e–86c — the paralysis complaint and the stingray, the
slave-boy demonstration, aporia (84a–c) as the productive numbing, and
anamnesis: knowledge recovered from within, not inserted.
