# Reading chunk 2: Nicomachean Ethics II.1-6 (habituation, hexis, the mean) — verify tradition-aristotle, kind-virtue's definition, and the cadence-as-habituation deferral

One office-hours sitting, at most 30 author-minutes. Chunk 2 of
`tactic-tradition-reading-program`; second leaf of the capture-doctrine path,
intended after `tactic-reading-chunk-1-plato-cave` (blocked_by encodes the
order). NE II.5–6 is also the textual foundation NE VI (chunk 5) presupposes.

## Text

Aristotle, *Nicomachean Ethics* II.1–6 — habituation (II.1–4: we become just
by doing just acts; the hexis is formed and kept by practice), virtue as
hexis, and the doctrine of the mean (II.5–6). Range widened from II.5–6 in
the 2026-07-09 /align-strategy second round to carry the
cadence-as-habituation deferral review; still one ~30-minute sitting.

## Questions to re-open against the text

- `intentions/kind-virtue.md` — does hexis/energeia actually say what
  "disposition held and exercised, never completed" claims?
- `intentions/kind-virtue.md` — is the `tension_with` machinery a fair
  rendering of the mean, given Aristotle's mean is within one virtue, not
  between two? This gap was papered over in the recording session — a real
  thing to check, not a formality.
- `intentions/tradition-aristotle.md` `chosen_over` — habituation over
  virtue-as-knowledge: does the text support the entry as recorded?
- `intentions/strategy-explicit-intent.md`, 2026-07-09 cadence-as-habituation
  deferral — does II.1–4 ground the recorded account (the recurring cadence
  as the graph's human-side habituation mechanism, the practice that
  maintains the disposition the arguments articulate; drift between sittings
  as the hexis decaying), or does the text place habituation's work
  elsewhere? Amend or ratify in the same sitting; the reading wins.

Two standing rules from `intentions/strategy-philosophical-grounding.md`
clarifications apply: the borrowed-articulation capture question and "the
reading wins" on any contradiction.
