# Reading chunk 20: Confessions VII.9-17 (the Platonist books) — verify conversio as periagoge received through Christian Platonism and open the personal-God tension

One office-hours sitting, at most 30 author-minutes. Chunk 20 of
`tactic-tradition-reading-program` (the full program and chunk list live in
that node's body). Second of the three Augustine verify chunks (19–21) for
`tradition-augustine`, recorded 2026-07-08 as an already-live deferral.

## Text

Augustine, *Confessions* VII.9–17 — the Platonist books: what Augustine found
in them (the turn inward and upward, the ascent) and what he did not; conversio
as periagoge received into Christian Platonism.

## Questions to re-open against the text

The 2026-07-08 record was drafted on Claude's account of the text plus the
author's unexamined Augustine background; this chunk checks both.

- `intentions/tradition-augustine.md` adopted — is conversio in Book VII
  actually periagoge received (a turning of the whole agent toward the good,
  not an information transfer), making the record a second witness alongside
  Republic VII rather than an independent doctrine?
- The personal-God clarification on the record — does VII's ascent turn toward
  the Good in Plato's sense or toward a person, and does "never toward a
  person or institution" (on `intentions/tradition-plato.md` and
  `intentions/virtue-philosophical-mobility.md`) mean only finite persons and
  institutions — liberators — or does it exclude Augustine's turning too?
- Does anything in VII's account of what the Platonist books *lacked* mark a
  divergence the record should carry?

Two standing rules from `intentions/strategy-philosophical-grounding.md`
clarifications apply: the borrowed-articulation capture question (this loop is
its exercise) and "the reading wins" on any contradiction.
