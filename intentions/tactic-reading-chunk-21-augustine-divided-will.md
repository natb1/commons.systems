# Reading chunk 21: Confessions VIII.5-12 (the divided will, the garden) — verify the phenomenology of the will's turning and answer the grace challenge to agency-never-lost

One office-hours sitting, at most 30 author-minutes. Chunk 21 of
`tactic-tradition-reading-program` (the full program and chunk list live in
that node's body). Third of the three Augustine verify chunks (19–21) for
`tradition-augustine`, recorded 2026-07-08 as an already-live deferral.

## Text

Augustine, *Confessions* VIII.5–12 — the divided will (VIII.5; VIII.9: the
will commands itself and is resisted), Ponticianus and the exempla, the garden
and *tolle lege* (VIII.12).

## Questions to re-open against the text

The 2026-07-08 record was drafted on Claude's account of the text plus the
author's unexamined Augustine background; this chunk checks both — and it
carries the record's sharpest held-open question.

- `intentions/tradition-augustine.md` adopted — does Book VIII ground "one is
  either internally compelled toward the light or chooses a liberator", with
  exempla and prompts as occasions rather than causes?
- The grace divergence (provisional on the record) — is the turning in VIII
  self-initiated in any sense that survives VIII.9, or does operative grace
  initiate it? If the latter: is the deferral to early Augustine with the
  grace doctrine refused, or does "agency is never lost" itself need amending
  — a cascade to `intentions/virtue-philosophical-mobility.md` and
  `intentions/tradition-plato.md`?
- The personal-God clarification continues here — toward whom or what does the
  garden turning turn?

Two standing rules from `intentions/strategy-philosophical-grounding.md`
clarifications apply: the borrowed-articulation capture question (this loop is
its exercise) and "the reading wins" on any contradiction.
