VII (517b-c, 592a-b in passing); Republic VI is the fuller treatment and is
otherwise uncited in the graph. The 2026-07-08 function clarifications on
`strategy-explicit-intent` lean on it.

- `intentions/tradition-plato.md` adopted — does VI support the anchor as
  recorded: one Good, external, never fully realizable, seen last and with
  difficulty, of which the graph is the author's best current realization?
- `intentions/strategy-explicit-intent.md` (2026-07-08 clarification) — the
  author's articulation "one Good, modeled imperfectly by constituent
  virtues, implemented by imperfect strategies": does the text support a
  constituent model of the Good, or does it cast the Good as the cause of
  intelligibility and being (508e-509b) rather than a whole with parts? If
  the latter, amend the clarification's wording, not its function.
- `intentions/virtue-philosophical-mobility.md` apex clarification — does VI
  move the held-open inside-the-graph apex question either way (candidate
  apex outside the graph, forest as unranked realization)?
