# Reading chunk 24: Phaedrus 274b-278b (writing as hypomnesis) — verify the covenant articulation of the graph as record of the author's internal model of the good

One curriculum chunk: ~30 author-minutes of independent reading; the review
session may span office-hours sittings (2026-07-08 session-bounds
clarification on `strategy-philosophical-grounding`). Chunk 24 of
`tactic-tradition-reading-program`, curriculum priority 16 — directly after
the deferral-review cluster (dialectic-method 13, sun-line 14, Protagoras
15), before the candidate batch (shifted to 17-24). Verify-style: reviews a
live deferral recorded 2026-07-09.

## Text

Plato, *Phaedrus* 274b–278b — the myth of Theuth and Thamus: writing as a
drug for reminding (hypomnesis), not for memory or wisdom; the written
word's appearance of wisdom — it cannot answer questions, says one thing
forever, and falls into the hands of those it was not written for; the
living discourse written in the soul, of which the written word is rightly
called an image (276a); writing as reminder for the one who already knows
(276d, 278a).

## Questions to re-open against the text

This chunk reviews a doctrine Claude drafted — the 2026-07-09 covenant
deferral: the graph as the imperfect, answerable record (hypomnema) of the
author's considered internal model of the good, the ground of type-b
interview authority.

- `intentions/strategy-explicit-intent.md`, 2026-07-09 covenant
  clarification — does the Phaedrus actually ground the covenant reading as
  recorded? Socrates' critique is of writing that *replaces* living
  knowledge; the covenant reading claims the graph escapes the critique by
  being answerable (the anti-calcification dialectic) and by reminding the
  one who already knows (the author, re-turned by type-b interviews). Is
  that escape textually honest, or does the passage cut against trusting
  any written record with authority over the present author?
- Does 276a's living-discourse/image relation ratify the recorded
  indirection (internal model as the realization, graph as its image), or
  does it demote the graph further than the record claims?
- On ratification: amend `tradition-plato` — new adopted entry for
  hypomnesis, and reconcile the 'best current realization' phrasing in the
  Form-of-the-Good entry to carry the indirection. On contradiction: the
  reading wins — amend the covenant clarification and the delegated-scope
  entry, and record the catch in
  `delegation-philosophical-articulation`'s divergence.contradictions.
