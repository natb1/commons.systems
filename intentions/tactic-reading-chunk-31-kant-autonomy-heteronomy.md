# Reading chunk 31: Kant, Groundwork section III (autonomy vs heteronomy) — verify tradition-kant's capture-as-heteronomy entry

## Text

Kant, *Groundwork of the Metaphysics of Morals*, section III — "Transition from
a metaphysics of morals to a critique of pure practical reason" (roughly
4:446–463): freedom as the key to the autonomy of the will, the reciprocity of
freedom and the moral law, and heteronomy as the will determined by an object
or interest outside itself. One work-section — the third Groundwork chunk
(4:429 and MM 6:434–437 are chunk 3). At most 30 author-minutes.
