# Reading chunk 32: Bentham, Introduction to the Principles of Morals and Legislation ch. 4 (the felicific calculus) — verify tradition-utilitarianism's single-scalar divergence

## Text

Bentham, *Introduction to the Principles of Morals and Legislation* ch. 4
("Value of a Lot of Pleasure or Pain, How to be Measured") — the felicific
calculus: the seven dimensions (intensity, duration, certainty, propinquity,
fecundity, purity, extent) and the procedure for summing pleasures and pains
into a single measure. One work-chapter — the Bentham text
`tradition-utilitarianism` cites as "the ancestor of every 'just score it'"
(Mill is chunk 9). At most 30 author-minutes.
