# Reading chunk 6: NE I.3 (precision of the subject) + I.8–10 (external goods) — verify the held-open apex warrant and the anti-Stoic bet, then revisit the apex question (capstone)

One office-hours sitting, at most 30 author-minutes. Chunk 6 of
`tactic-tradition-reading-program` (working-order position 6). These texts
underwrite holding the apex question open, and the anti-Stoic bet on
externals.

## Text

Aristotle, *Nicomachean Ethics* I.3 — precision of the subject. Aristotle,
*Nicomachean Ethics* I.8–10 — external goods. Plus NE I.7 (the ergon
argument), added 2026-07-13 to carry the chunk-2 function deferral.

Plus NE I.1–2, added 2026-07-18 to carry the chunk-5 architectonic flag; the
sitting also runs the eudaimonia hexis-vs-energeia test (I.7–8).
## Questions to re-open against the text

The 2026-07-04/05 interview resolutions were made on Claude's account of the
text; this chunk checks the account.

- Both roots (`intentions/virtue-philosophical-mobility.md`,
  `intentions/virtue-respect-for-persons.md`) — *"Does the root forest
  conceal a tacit apex — 'agency as an end'?"*: the HELD-OPEN question and
  the round's standing review trigger. NE I.3 is the recorded warrant for
  holding it open (a documented choice of Aristotle's
  precision-of-the-subject over Kant's demand for system), so this chunk
  decides whether that warrant is real.
- `intentions/tradition-aristotle.md` — does I.8–10 support the anti-Stoic
  bet on externals as recorded?
- Deferral from chunk 2 (2026-07-13): the ergon — II.6's "performs its
  function well" (1106a15–24) was read by the author as alignment with a
  separate, independent ideal external to the possessor. Does I.7's function
  argument (1097b24–1098a18) support that, or is the ergon immanent — the
  thing's own characteristic activity, form-as-actuality rather than a
  separate paradigm? Decide whether the external-ideal reading is a
  divergence; the successor question (can the ideal be fully internalized)
  is homed on chunk 5.

- Deferral from chunk 5 (2026-07-18): I.1–2 (the architectonic) — R13's second
  half: graph-serving code and tooling as subordinate technai under an
  architectonic ordering (I.1's ends-hierarchy, I.2's politikē); verify at the
  text.
- Deferral from chunk 5 (2026-07-18): the eudaimonia hexis-vs-energeia test
  (I.7–8, possession vs use) — the author's finale phrase "the hexis of
  eudaimonia" is flagged for exactly this test: is eudaimonia an energeia,
  not a hexis?
- Deferral from chunk 5 (2026-07-18): R12's ergon grounding — the graph's
  three-function reading (alignment over long horizons; forming right desire
  toward the spoudaios; shareable periagoge-shaped store) claims I.7's ergon
  frame; verify.

**Capstone rule:** after chunks 1–6 are done (wherever they fall in the
working order), revisit the apex question itself.

clarifications apply: the borrowed-articulation capture question (this loop is
