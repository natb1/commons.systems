# Displace platform listening with the owned audio player (born-parked)

## Context

`strategy-recover-attention`'s own stated failing edge — the owned audio player
has not displaced platform listening — is the strategy's only signal-path work
this round, and it is human-owned. It cannot be planned autonomously because the
concrete shape of the displacement is an author decision, not something derivable
from the graph. Born-parked to `office_hours` on the 2026-07-07 `/align-tactics`
round (consumed from its prior retained-draft state).

## Decision the author must make

Pick the concrete displacement approach (or scope a first ≤30-author-minute
slice of one):

- **(a) Content/ingestion pipeline** — make the author's desired long-form audio
  (podcasts, lectures, audiobooks) reachable inside the owned player, so there is
  something to listen to without the platform app.
- **(b) Listening-habit change** — default a defined listening category to the
  owned player and treat the platform service as a deliberate exception.
- **(c) Capability-gap close** — identify and close a specific capability the
  platform player still has that the owned one lacks and that keeps the author on
  the platform.

Once the author records the chosen approach (via `/align-strategy`, or by shaping
this into a concrete tactic), a later `/align-tactics` round decomposes and plans
the claude-eligible parts. Until then this node is not selectable — it carries no
implement plan by design.
