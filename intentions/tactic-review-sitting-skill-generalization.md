# Generalize the office-hours review sitting beyond reading chunks: mode-A source re-validation for non-text sources (delegation exercise, world-state/condition checks) and mode-B context-broadening confirmation sittings for author-owned doctrine, extending the /reading-review machinery

Retained draft from the 2026-07-09 /align-strategy review-curriculum round —
input to a future /align-tactics pass; not yet a plan. Plan only after
tactic-reading-review-skill lands (same rule as
tactic-reading-review-candidate-extension — this extends that skill's file).

## Context

Both curriculum modes are type-b-style sittings differing in what the author
collects beforehand (strategy-graph-review-curriculum clarification 3):

- **Mode A beyond texts**: reading chunks exist; delegation-exercise sittings
  (collect: the exercised recovery path; stamp last_exercised, honor
  review_window — tactic-delegation-review-windows) and world-state/condition
  sittings (collect: the current world state behind an
  attributes.conditions entry — tactic-condition-review-sweep's substance,
  rehoming pending at tactic-align-audit-legacy-review) do not.
- **Mode B (the new machinery)**: confirmation sittings for author-owned
  doctrine — collect broadened context of a reading, exercise, or world
  state (recursively broadening, as the philosophical curriculum broadens
  reading context), then re-affirm or amend the prior author-owned assertion.
  The periagoge/recall-first-delta rules apply: the record at origin/main is
  the fixed object, the author articulates first.
- Every sitting ends by expanding the frontier: the broadened context names
  the next frontier entries (born-parked nodes, same graph-commit) — this
  recursive expansion is what gives author-owned nodes periodic coverage.
- The curriculum mechanism itself is enrolled: these skills and the strategy
  are subject to the same review (strategy clarification 4).

Out of scope: /reading-review's existing reading-chunk flow (unchanged);
enrollment at record time (tactic-align-curriculum-maintenance); the coverage
table (tactic-review-curriculum-coverage-sensor).
