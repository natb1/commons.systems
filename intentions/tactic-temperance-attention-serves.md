# Evaluate serves edges from the attention-recovery strategies to virtue-temperance where the engineered-appetite reading holds

Finalized born-parked 2026-07-06 /align-tactics round. Author-owned graph-edit
sitting, at most 30 author-minutes; not a sweep — each edge is a per-strategy
judgment.

## Task

For each candidate strategy, decide whether it gains a `serves` edge to
`virtue-temperance` **alongside** its existing roots (never replacing them):

- `intentions/strategy-recover-attention.md` — owned reading/listening tools;
  the appetite reading plausibly holds (feeds as engineered appetite).
- `intentions/strategy-attention-surface.md` — the office-hours graph surface;
  check whether its object is attention-consumption at all or only tooling.
- `intentions/strategy-realign-attachments.md` — swapping engagement-funded
  attachments; the engineered-appetite reading is near its core claim.

## Criterion

The edge lands only where the strategy's own object is appetite regulation in
virtue-temperance's recorded sense — "engagement feeds are engineered
appetite, so the attention-recovery fight is appetite regulation"
(`intentions/virtue-temperance.md`, rationale and the scope clarification). A
strategy that merely touches attention infrastructure does not qualify.
Precedent for the edge shape: `intentions/strategy-nourishment.md` already
serves `virtue-temperance`.

## Recording

Land the chosen edges as frontmatter `serves` additions on the candidate
strategies via `graph-commit`, with a dated note in each amended strategy's
rationale. Declining an edge is also a result — record the declination and why
in this node's body when closing it out.

Reading chunk 4 (NE III.10–12, sōphrosynē — not yet broken out of
`tactic-tradition-reading-program`) later checks whether the attention
extension is citation or the graph's own extension. That affects how an edge's
rationale is documented (extension, not citation), not whether this evaluation
