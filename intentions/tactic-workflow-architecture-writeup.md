# Architecture writeup for the dispatch workflow on the owned blog — how the intention graph and its harness are built

Born-parked author voice work: the ungated half split out of
tactic-workflow-entry-point by the 2026-07-11 /align-tactics round, per
strategy-distribute-workflow's 2026-07-06 clarification — publishing how the
workflow is built, on the owned blog, is ungated voice under
strategy-recover-publishing and can ship any time; only the
standing-entry-point half waits for the tier-3 declaration.

## First chunk (≤30 author-minutes)

Decide the piece's frame and outline at office-hours and record them here:

- Audience: practitioners evaluating the workflow.
- Candidate scope: the intention graph as the product, the harness as its
  reference consumer (strategy-data-structure-first's layered claim); the
  align skill family; the router/phase model; what forking actually takes.

Drafting within the recorded outline is then delegable claude work — a later
/align-tactics round plans it behind a blog-copy approval gate (the
tactic-readme-copy-approval pattern), since blog posts are in-scope copy
under strategy-author-approved-copy.
