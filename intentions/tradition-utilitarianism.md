  measurement without a single scalar of value. This record explains the design
  to a future reader who asks 'why not just score it?': because a graph that can
  total its virtues has already decided they are commensurable, and
  commensurability is what makes persons and dispositions tradeable. Recorded
  2026-07-04 interview."
