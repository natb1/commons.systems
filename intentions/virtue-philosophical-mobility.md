rationale: >-
  Mobility is recoverability, not retention. The test is never "how detached
  am I?" but "can I still move when I choose?" — atrophy of the ability to
  move is the danger, not dependence itself. Permanent delegation and skill
  atrophy are expected and beneficial: they buy attention at the strategic
  level. Unrecoverable atrophy is capture.


  Mobility IS the discernment that monitors every delegation for divergence
  and judges when recovery is necessary. It is exercised only through its two
  constituent virtues — virtue-progressive-detachment (keep recovery paths
  real) and virtue-alignment-of-attachments (keep delegatees aligned) — held
  in tension: forced detachment is dogma, and dogma requires power to
  maintain.


  This judgment is the one capability that can never be delegated to atrophy:
  I can forget how to smelt copper, never how to notice I have been captured.
  And it cannot fully verify itself — capture corrupts the sensor that
  detects capture, because imported language reshapes how problems are
  perceived. Its calibration is therefore external: adversarial review and a
  community of fellow practitioners.


  Applied reflexively: this project is itself an institution and may ratchet
  up when that serves its purpose. Growth is not the pathology; the inability
  to reverse it is. The project must stay organized so that standing down is
  what happens by inaction, never an act of will that a ratcheted institution
  never performs.
