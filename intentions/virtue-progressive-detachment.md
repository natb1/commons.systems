rationale: >-
  Keep every recovery path real, bounded, and un-gated. Complete detachment is
  impossible and not the goal; permanent delegation and skill atrophy are
  expected and beneficial — they buy attention at the strategic level. What must
  never atrophy is the path back: for each delegation, a recovery route
  (rebuild, re-host, substitute, relearn) whose cost stays bounded and whose
  knowledge is not gated by the party I would recover from. An unexercised
  recovery path is a hope, not a path.


  The method is progressive: map the dependencies, sort which serve me from
  which feed on me, and incrementally take ownership where the cost-benefit
  favors it. Agentic coding shifted that calculation by making individual
  software ownership feasible where it used to require institutional platforms.
  The construction-tool-vs-runtime distinction is this virtue applied to the
  coding agent: outputs are local-first and persist without the agent —
  iteration slows, and what is built decays only at the rate its substrate
  drifts.


  Atrophy is safe down to the layer where the recovery meta-skill lives, and
  dangerous below it: the capability to read, evaluate, and reason about what
  the delegatee produces is the floor under every recovery path.


  The Aristotelian triple, made explicit at chunk 2 (NE II.6-7, 2026-07-13):
  this virtue is itself a mean in its field, the irreversibility axis. Its
  excess is forced detachment — dogma, detachment pursued as retention-zero,
  which requires power to maintain; its deficiency is gated or unexercised
  recovery paths — capture by irreversibility. The balance against its sibling
  is the cross-guard recorded on kind-virtue's chunk-2 clarification, not this
  internal mean.
