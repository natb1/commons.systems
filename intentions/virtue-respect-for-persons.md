rationale: >-
  Treat every person as an end, never merely as a means. This is a sibling root
  to virtue-philosophical-mobility, not a child of it: mobility alone cannot
  ground it — a perfectly mobile actor could keep his own exits warm while
  capturing others. The two are directions of one commitment, agency as an end:
  mobility is its self-directed face (do not let your own agency be annexed),
  respect its other-directed face (never annex anyone else's). Neither derives
  from the other without remainder.


  Respect is what forbids ever being the captor, and it dictates the shape of
  everything offered outward: transfer capability, not dependency; selection
  over conversion; migration freedom as a design requirement, not a courtesy.
  The nearest persons are equally in scope: the household that shares
  attachments. Respect governs inward as well as outward — moves on shared
  delegations are made with the persons who share them, never for them, and the
  practice is offered to family exactly as to strangers: never imposed.
  Continuity is the same inward scope: shared data and the identity root stay
  reachable by the household when the author is unavailable — an archive or a
  root only the author can open treats them as dependents, not persons.
  Promotion of the practice is over-determined — mobility justifies it
  instrumentally (every practitioner strengthens the recovery substrate and
  calibrates discernment), respect justifies it intrinsically — but its METHODS
  answer to this virtue alone: no lock-in, no dark patterns, no conversion,
  useful artifacts that never require philosophical buy-in.

  - question: Is this virtue a Kantian constraint or an Aristotelian disposition?
    answer: "Both, in layers (recorded 2026-07-04 interview). The PROHIBITION is
      Kantian and exceptionless: never be the captor — no lock-in, no dark
      patterns, no conversion. It is a side-constraint, not a weighable good:
      nothing in the graph is balanced against it, which is why this node
      carries no tension_with — explicit doctrine rather than accident (see
      attributes.inviolable); the kinship field that once marked the mobility
      relation was retired 2026-07-09, the two-faces relation living in this
      node's rationale, its one auditable home. HOW to respect well — when to
      promote, how hard to offer the practice to family, what counts as imposing
      — is Aristotelian phronesis. Kant for the floor, Aristotle for the
      practice; the split is recorded as chosen_over entries on both tradition
      records."
  - question: A household member asks not to be consulted on shared-delegation moves
      — does accepting that delegation honor 'with, never for' or breach it?
    answer: "Split by the irreversibility axis (recorded 2026-07-04 interview). On
      routine moves, accepting IS respect: autonomy includes the right to
      delegate one's voice, and mandated participation would impose the practice
      this node forbids imposing. On irreversible, personhood-level moves — the
      identity root, the archive, anything the continuity clause covers —
      consultation stays mandatory: Kant's servility argument (Metaphysics of
      Morals 6:434-437) holds that consent does not validate self-abandonment,
      so a blanket 'never ask me' cannot cover moves that would render the
      person a dependent. The Aristotelian practice around the floor: a
      lightweight periodic re-offer, so the delegation stays visibly revocable."
