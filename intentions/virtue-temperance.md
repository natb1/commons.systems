rationale: >-
  A disposition, never completed: wanting movement, nourishment, rest, and
  stimulation in the right amount at the right time. Aristotle's sōphrosynē (NE
  III.10-12) supplies the structure; Plato's ordered soul (Republic IV) supplies
  the content — appetite ruled by judgment. The scope is deliberately broader
  than the body: engagement feeds are engineered appetite, so the
  attention-recovery fight is appetite regulation, and this root names what
  those strategies are actually exercising. Attention-domain strategies may add
  serves edges in later rounds; nothing is re-rooted today.


  Why a root and not a child of virtue-philosophical-mobility: the body is the
  substrate every other virtue is exercised through — discernment runs on sleep,
  and no recovery path rebuilds the organism — but health is held as an end, not
  derived from mobility. The same sibling-root argument recorded on
  virtue-respect-for-persons applies: mobility gives an instrumental reason to
  be temperate, and neither derives from the other without remainder.


  The internal tension is real but not yet split into constituent children:
  discipline against recovery — training load against rest, restriction against
  nourishment. Balanced, not summed; a maximized discipline is failure, not
  progress. If constituents are ever recorded they carry tension_with. Rooted
  2026-07-04 interview (strategy-philosophical-grounding, first round), from the
  finding that exercising, eating right, and a sleep schedule aligned to no
  existing virtue.
