## Answer

Yes: a ratified answer under a fresh review still carries a stage, with
only this Answer section to satisfy the requirement -- no Disposition or
Account needed, since an '## Answer' is now enough on its own. The
recommendation adopts the node as it stands, so there are no alternatives
on the table and no '## Recommendation' fence.
