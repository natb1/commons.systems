# Phase 0.5 Marketing Plan — Program, Outreach, Website
**Filling every evening: the demand side of the max-utilization pilot**
*Aug 28, 2026. Companion to `pilot/phase-0.5-spec.md` (Amendment A), `pilot/space-finishing-plan.md`, `schedule.md` Revision 6. Prospect base: T2 census in `validation/data.md` (34 groups, ≥13 with documented venue pain).*

**Goal restated:** near-full utilization of the club space — a session most evenings (~7 slots/week, ~28–30/month at steady state), daytime private. Utilization is the validation instrument: max slots filled = best market evidence, most conversion candidates, deepest list for the fundraising long shot, and the strongest community-service narrative for grants and subsidized financing.

---

## 1. Program design

### 1.1 The slot grid

One evening slot per day, **6:30–9:30 PM, hard quiet-by-10** (reset 9:30–10). Weekend afternoon slots (Sat/Sun 1–4 PM) open later as bonus capacity once evenings are >70% filled — daytime weekday stays private. Each slot holds one group of ≤8–10.

### 1.2 The portfolio: hosted clubs + house programs

Filling ~29 slots/month purely with external recurring groups needs 10–14 distinct groups (a weekly group = 4 slots/mo, biweekly = 2, monthly = 1) — more than the T2 warm list alone will yield early. So the program runs two engines:

- **Hosted clubs (the validation payload):** external recurring groups with their own identity and leadership — book clubs, TTRPG tables, writing groups, board-game groups, craft circles. These are what convert at graduation; they are the priority claim on any slot.
- **House programs (fill + reps + brand):** operator-run open-format nights that fill whatever the hosted clubs don't, keep the calendar alive during ramp, and generate walk-in-equivalent discovery. Candidates play to the operator's own hand: an **open TTRPG table** (the operator already GMs publicly — Shadowdark at No Land Beyond — this is a proven program with a portable audience and it makes the No Land Beyond referral conversation reciprocal), a **silent reading / writing night** (lowest host effort — critical for sustainability), a **board-game open table**, a **film night**, and a monthly **print/zine salon** that puts the Odd Prints half of the brand to work — the stairwell gallery plus a table night around prints, zines, and book arts.

House programs are honest fill, not padding: they get logged like everything else, they test formats, and their regulars are recruitable into clubs. But **graduation-ask metrics count hosted clubs only** — the operator cannot convert himself.

**Steady-state target mix (aspirational, atop the spec §5 floors, which stand unchanged):** ~3 weekly hosted clubs (12 slots/mo) + ~4 biweekly (8) + ~3–4 monthly (3–4) + 1–2 weekly house programs (4–8) ≈ **27–32 slots/month**. Utilization metric: **slots filled ÷ slots offered**, logged monthly; aspiration ≥50% by month 3, ≥80% by month 5.

### 1.3 Ramp discipline

Do not launch at 7/week. Event #0 is a **neighbors' open house** (see §2.4). Then: weeks 1–2 → 2 events/wk; add ~1/wk as coordination routine solidifies; 7/wk by ~month 3–4. The spec's month-2 checkpoint (fill ≥4/mo floor) and month-4 checkpoint (diagnose) are unchanged; the burnout stop-rule (spec §8) now matters more, and the silent-reading-night lever exists precisely because some filled evenings must cost the host almost nothing.

## 2. Community outreach

### 2.1 The offer (unchanged in substance, bigger in ambition)

"I'm opening a café with private club rooms. While we build toward it, I'm hosting **founding groups** at my place, free — a private, staged club room, tea service, a standing slot that's yours." Easy yes; honest; makes the graduation ask the natural next chapter. Strictly free — no fees, no cost-sharing, no suggested donations; potluck norm; operator serves tea.

### 2.2 Channels, in priority order

1. **Direct recruitment from the T2 census** — the ≥13 named prospects with documented venue pain first (Panera backrooms, RSVP rationing, noise-failed venues, waitlisted home groups, the forming D&D group with no venue). Personal, specific, one group at a time.
2. **Referral loops** — every retained group is asked once, ~month 2 of their tenure: "what other group should have a night here?" D&J and Canton Games / No Land Beyond conversations (already scheduled, #6c) now carry a reciprocal shape: they get overflow relief and cross-promotion; the pilot gets referrals.
3. **Platform presence where groups already look:** Meetup (a "host your group" listing, not per-event spam), r/baltimore and Baltimore Discords, Facebook groups, Nextdoor for the neighborhood radius. The census work (Discord/FB/Reddit, carried from R3) doubles as channel mapping — census and outreach are the same motion now.
4. **Neighborhood and institutional:** Little Italy neighborhood association, nearby Pratt branch bulletin boards (flyers live where the audience reads — no signage at the house itself), universities' club offices in spring.
5. **The website + mailing list** (§3) — the persistent surface everything else points at.

**Outreach volume math, precommitted:** filling the funnel to 10–14 groups likely requires touching **~40–60 groups** over the window (expect ~1 in 4 to reach a first session, ~2 in 3 of those to stick). The T2 census must therefore keep growing — refresh monthly (already in the rhythm), target +5–8 newly identified groups/month. If genuine outreach at this volume can't fill slots, that is the meaningful negative evidence the spec's asymmetry logic wants.

### 2.3 Pipeline stages (log every group at a stage, monthly, in `validation/data.md`)

prospect → contacted → toured/first session → recurring (2+ sessions on cadence) → retained (month-3+ hold) → **graduation converter**. The pipeline table is the rooms-bar evidence package in embryo; keeping it current is what makes G1 assembly cheap.

### 2.4 Neighbors as first audience

Event #0 is for the block: open house, tea, see the space, hear the plan, considerate-parking cards handed out. Neighbors who have been inside are neighbors who phone you, not the city, when something's off. This is #25 community-relations work done early and in person; at 7 nights/week the block's goodwill is load-bearing.

## 3. Website (operator designs, develops, hosts)

Purpose, in order: **(1) credibility** — a group leader checking out "some person's house event" finds an establishment instead; **(2) brand vibe** — the visual register the commercial site will inherit; **(3) funnel** — one clear action: *request a night for your group*; **(4) the list** — every future asset (opening launch, fundraising long shot, grant narrative) compounds on the mailing list.

**Not on the site:** prices (nothing is for sale — keeps the no-commerce posture clean), the street address (shared on confirmed RSVP only — safety + no-signage posture), e-commerce of any kind, hype about the future café beyond the honest founding-groups story.

### 3.1 Brand notes

Name in full: **Albemarle Tea & Odd Prints**. Register: club-room-above-a-library — bookish, warm, slightly odd, print-literate; closer to a letterpress poster than a startup landing page. Typography does the heavy lifting (a serif with real italics + a workmanlike grotesque); palette from the rooms themselves (library green/oxblood accent, warm paper, 2700K light in every photo). Photography is the whole argument — **the site launches only after the staged-space photo shoot** (build critical path, finishing plan §4). Print aesthetic is on-brand to the point of strategy: the house guide cards, parking cards, and site should look like one print family — Odd Prints making itself.

### 3.2 Information architecture (5 pages, static)

| Page | Job | Content |
|---|---|---|
| Home | The vibe in 5 seconds | Full-bleed club-room photo, one-line premise ("A club room above a library-café — currently meeting in Little Italy"), two CTAs: *bring your group* / *join the list* |
| The Club | What happens here | Format menu (book club, TTRPG, writing night, games, film, print salon), the standing-slot idea, house norms (≤10, quiet by 10, potluck, free while we build), calendar view (public: what nights are taken, not who) |
| Bring Your Group | The funnel | Short request form (group, format, size, cadence, contact) → email; expectation-setting ("we'll have tea and talk") |
| The Future Café | The honest arc | Founding-groups story: what's being built, what founding groups get at the real site, mailing-list signup for the journey; later, this page carries any fundraising campaign |
| About / Contact | Credibility | Who the operator is, why this exists, contact; no address |

### 3.3 Build notes

Static site + form endpoint + embedded read-only calendar is fully sufficient; the operator chooses the stack. Mailing list via any lightweight provider from day 1 — the list is the asset, the tool is fungible. Structure and copy can be drafted in September (copy drafts on request — say the word), photos late October, **launch before first invitations go out** so every outreach message has a link that does the credibility work.

## 4. The fundraising long shot (recorded, not banked)

Max utilization also maximizes the audience for community fundraising toward commercial capital — treated exactly like grants: a possibility to be positioned for, never a plan input. Posture: **no money at or around the house, ever** (preserves the pilot's legal cleanliness and the graduation signal). What positioning costs nothing now: the mailing list, the documented community story (event log + photos), and the Future Café page as the future campaign surface. Instrument selection (community crowdfunding, MD-eligible community investment structures, a straight Kickstarter for site build-out) is a G2-era question — folded into `strategy/financing-alternatives.md` when it becomes live. If a campaign ever runs, it funds the **commercial site**, is framed as such, and follows entity formation — contributions relate to the LLC, not the residence.

## 5. What this plan changes elsewhere

- Utilization aspiration targets (≥50% mo-3, ≥80% mo-5) sit **on top of** the pilot spec §5 floors; floors and graduation bars are untouched.
- Outreach volume (touch 40–60 groups; census +5–8/month) becomes a standing-rhythm line (schedule Rev 6).
- Website build lands in the prep phase with photos gating launch.
- House programs get logged with a `house` flag in the event log so hosted-club metrics stay clean.
