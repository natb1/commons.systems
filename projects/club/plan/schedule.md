## How to read this

Five gates. A gate is a decision you can't unmake cheaply — the schedule makes the right work land *before* each one. **Everything before G2 is now deliberately cheap** (the residence finish-out being house-side spend, not venture capital).

| Gate | Decision | Target |
|---|---|---|
| **Pilot graduation** *(D16-r)* | Free pilot complete (≥6 mo); graduation ask executed; conversion evidence in hand | ~May 2027 |
| **G1** | Finalist market chosen — **DUAL GATE (D10): room-demand bar AND café-count bar, equal weight; failing either kills that market** · + nonprofit trigger test, pass 1 | ~May–Jun 2027 |
| **G2** | Building chosen; LOI signed; lease-vs-buy structure set · + trigger test re-run on real numbers (both arms — plan §9) | ~Jul–Aug 2027 |
| **G3** | Lease (or financing-contingent contract) executed | ~Q4 2027 |
| **G4** | Financing/TI closed; permits filed | ~Q1–Q2 2028 |
| **G5** | Open | ~mid–late 2028 |

### Precommitted bars (written before the data arrives — do not renegotiate after)

- **Café bar (G1):** seasonally-adjusted counts supporting ≥ ~90 tx/day (Little Italy) / ~72 (SN/HT) — 90% of the modeled 100/80. The multi-season count series (fall haircut ×0.75–0.8, winter raw) replaces the single-snapshot read; winter counts weigh heaviest.
- **Rooms bar (G1, per market):** ≥2 recurring groups with deposits or signed recurring-booking agreements AND ≥4 additional money-backed session pre-bookings — all at §1a recreational rates. **Evidence sources (D16-r): graduation-ask conversions (attributed to the market each group commits to migrating to) + independent deposits. Free pilot attendance counts for nothing here.** Office/membership interest is upside evidence only. *(If a Phase 0.75 interim option is adopted, its paid recurring bookings join the instrument list by written amendment at adoption — `pilot/interim-phase-options.md` §7.2.)*
- **Pilot bars (graduation, from the spec §5–6):** fill ≥4 hosted events/mo by month 2; 3–5 recurring groups by month 3 holding to month 6; ≥3–4 formats hosted; graduation conversion ≥50% of retained groups = strong, <25% = genuine price-problem signal. *(Utilization aspirations — ≥50% mo-3, ≥80% mo-5 of offered slots — sit above these floors and are fill-rate findings, not pass/fail.)*
- **Stop rules:** post-haircut counts <70 tx/day in both markets → stop; café co-anchor wrong-sized. Rooms bar failed in both markets at G1 → rooms fail as a revenue service; §9 trigger second arm before any LOI. Both → restructure or stop per `strategy/entity-structure.md`. **Pilot-specific:** a pilot that fills easily but converts nowhere at graduation is §9-trigger evidence pointing nonprofit-first (plan §9); a pilot that can't fill free slots despite genuine outreach weighs against §1a at G1 — but only the gate instruments fire the trigger. High utilization via house programs is operator output, not market pull — judge demand on the hosted-club pipeline.

## Schedule risks & re-anchors

- **Build slip (new, Rev 6):** the bathroom is the critical-path item — no functioning guest bath, no events. If build hours fall below ~10/wk for two consecutive weeks, either compress scope (Tier 1 minimums only) or buy a Tier-3 labor day; do not let prep drift past mid-November, or the ramp loses the pre-holiday window.
- **Burnout at 7 nights/week (elevated, Rev 6):** the aspiration is a ceiling, not a quota — the spec's burnout stop-rule governs; low-effort formats (silent reading night) are the pressure valve. If the weekly rhythm collapses for a month, treat it as a real signal, not a slip.
- **Momentum over the long runway (D16-r):** the standing rhythm and pilot cadence are the mitigation; the month-2/month-4 checkpoints keep the pilot honest.
- **Inventory turnover (accepted, D16-r):** candidate buildings will change before G2; tour to learn markets, chase nothing pre-verdict; re-run the building search from then-current inventory at G2.
- **Pilot fill failure despite genuine outreach** (now at precommitted volume: 40–60 groups touched) → diagnose at checkpoints (offer? formats? house?); weighs against §1a at G1 but only gate instruments fire the trigger.
- **Pilot fills but graduation converts <25%** → genuine price-problem signal; §9 trigger analysis before any LOI (community value without willingness to pay points nonprofit-first).
- **Post-haircut counts <70 tx/day in both markets** → stop; the café co-anchor is wrong-sized. (Bar precommitted above.)
- **Architect flags compliance/occupancy cost >~$120K on both leads** → widen the building search (sprinkler retrofits alone can eat the old $75K number).
- **Neighbor complaint at the pilot** → adjust/pause per spec §8; the block's goodwill outranks the pilot — doubly so at every-evening cadence.
- **SBLP cadence** → informational until the buy option is live; no round is worth accelerating capital past the pilot verdict.
- **Trigger test fails at G1 or G2 (either arm)** → the venture is factually a charity or a no: pause site control, restructure nonprofit-first per `strategy/entity-structure.md`, or stop.
