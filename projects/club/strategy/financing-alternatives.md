# Lower-cash / debt-heavier paths — analysis (Aug 27, 2026)

*Question from operator: is there a path to starting with less cash and more reliance on financing? Answer: partially — for the asset side only, and at a real cost. Summary below; feeds open-questions #13 (DHCD/SBLP consultation) and #17 (TI negotiation).*

## The structural answer

The ~$150K splits into two different kinds of cash, and financing substitutes for them very differently:

1. **Assets (~$85K fit-out/FF&E/deposits)** — debt-financeable in principle. Lenders, landlords, and lessors all finance things with salvage value or that attach to their own building.
2. **Working capital + Year-1 ramp (~$50–65K)** — effectively NOT debt-financeable for this plan. Borrowing to fund operating losses on a venture whose own conservative base case is negative (−$13K LI owner comp) converts the Lean scenario from a contained, survivable loss into insolvency with debt outstanding. The reserve stays cash, first-claim, whatever the financing structure.

So the realistic floor is roughly **$85–100K cash** (reserve + deposits + un-financeable soft costs), with **$40–65K of the asset side financed** — not a $30K-down business.

## Instruments (verified Aug 27, 2026)

- **NBW/SSBCI (DHCD)**: the SSBCI NBW tier is now **$350K–$5M; loans under $350K are routed to CDFI partner programs.** Implication for #13: at this project's scale the acceleration lender is likely a **CDFI**, not DHCD directly — update the consultation target list. NBW terms note *personal guarantees and collateral required*.
- **CDFIs (the realistic tier)**: Baltimore Community Lending; **Baltimore Business Lending** (explicitly for startups lacking equity/collateral, via micro-lender collaboration — contact: 410-319-0732); LEDC (SBA microlender serving Baltimore).
- **SBA 7(a)**: startups (<2 yrs) require **≥10% owner equity injection**; lenders commonly want more for food service, and underwrite projections — a plan whose honest base case is negative will struggle to underwrite until gate evidence exists. Personal guarantee ≥20% owners, unavoidable.
- **SBA microloan**: ≤$50K through intermediaries; startup-friendly; PG still standard.
- **Kiva**: ~$15K, 0%, no fees, no PG/collateral — small but the cheapest dollars available (~$5K/yr principal over 3 yrs).
- **Landlord TI / free rent / second-generation space**: the cheapest real financing and already plan #17 — TI amortized into rent hits occupancy, not a loan file, and carries no PG. A former-restaurant space can cut the $85K build-out dramatically; this is the highest-leverage "financing" lever.
- **Equipment leasing** (espresso, refrigeration): shifts ~$15–25K of FF&E off day-one cash at lease rates.
- **Community capital**: regulation crowdfunding (Honeycomb-style community debt) and **prepaid standing-slot club memberships** (precedent-research §7.4) — capital that doubles as demand validation; conceptually an extension of the G1 deposit instrument. *(See also `pilot/marketing-plan.md` §4 — the pilot's mailing list and community story are the positioning for any future community-capital campaign; and `pilot/interim-phase-options.md` §6 — prepaid founding memberships as a Phase 0.75 bridge instrument.)*

## The cost math

Rule of thumb: **each $10K of CDFI-grade debt (~9–11%, 7–10 yr) costs ~$1.5–2K/yr of owner comp** — against a base case already at −$13K (LI). Examples: $50K @ 10%/7yr ≈ $10K/yr; $50K @ 9%/10yr ≈ $7.6K/yr. Borrowing $60K raises the LI gate-clearing gap from ~$43K to ~$53–55K; ~$22–25K of debt cancels the contribution of one filled weekly evening session (+$4.4K/yr).

## The guarantee problem (D7 conflict)

Nearly every instrument above except TI, leasing, Kiva, and prepaid memberships requires a **personal guarantee**. The Albemarle house is never pledged, but a PG makes it reachable by judgment if the venture fails with debt outstanding — the D7 firewall holds legally only until a guaranteed default. The cash-funded plan caps the worst case at ~$150K and the house is untouchable; a debt-heavy plan does not.

## Recommended posture

Extend D8's logic (debt = acceleration, never assumption) from the buy path to the lease path:

- **Pre-gates (now)**: take only no-PG financing — negotiate TI/free rent hard, lease equipment, Kiva, prepaid club slots. This can trim day-one cash toward ~$100–115K without changing the risk shape.
- **Post-G1/G2 (evidence in hand)**: if the gates clear, that evidence is exactly what a CDFI underwrites — then modest debt ($30–50K) to accelerate fit-out scope or deepen reserve is rational, and the debt service is carried by a validated, not hoped-for, revenue line.
- **Never**: debt-fund the working-capital reserve, or sign a PG before the dual gate has produced evidence the base case beats −$13K.
